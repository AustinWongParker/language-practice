# Lesson 41 · Past time words
**Week 7 · The past (preterite)** · about 30 min · [[Spanish 8-Week Course|Course overview]] · ← [[40 - Preterite irregulars - estar, poder, decir, ver, dar|Lesson 40]] · [[42 - Week 7 review - what did you do yesterday|Lesson 42]] →

> [!tip] Goal
> Place events in the past and tell them in order.

## 1 · Learn (10 min)
| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **ayer** | yesterday |
| **anteayer** | the day before yesterday |
| **anoche** | last night |
| **el lunes pasado** | last Monday |
| **la semana pasada** | last week |
| **el mes pasado** | last month |
| **el año pasado** | last year |
| **hace dos días** | two days ago |

**Hace + amount of time** = ago: **hace una semana** (a week ago), **hace mucho tiempo** (a long time ago).

**Telling a story:** **primero** → **luego** → **después** → **de repente** (suddenly) → **al final** (in the end). **Por fin** = finally, at last.

**New words**
