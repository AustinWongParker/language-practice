# Lesson 37 · Preterite - regular -AR verbs
**Week 7 · The past (preterite)** · about 30 min · [[Spanish 8-Week Course|Course overview]] · ← [[36 - Week 6 review - conversation practice|Lesson 36]] · [[38 - Preterite - regular -ER and -IR verbs|Lesson 38]] →

> [!tip] Goal
> Say what you did with regular -ar verbs.

## 1 · Learn (10 min)
The **preterite** is for finished actions in the past: what happened.

| | hablar | ending |
|---|---|---|
| yo | **hablé** | -é |
| tú | **hablaste** | -aste |
| él / ella / usted | **habló** | -ó |
| nosotros | **hablamos** | -amos |
| vosotros | **hablasteis** | -asteis |
| ellos / ustedes | **hablaron** | -aron |

- The accent matters: **hablo** (I speak) vs **habló** (he spoke).
- **Nosotros** is the same as the present: **hablamos** = we speak / we spoke. Context tells you which.
- Spelling changes in **yo** only: **-car → -qué** (busqué), **-gar → -gué** (llegué), **-zar → -cé** (empecé).
