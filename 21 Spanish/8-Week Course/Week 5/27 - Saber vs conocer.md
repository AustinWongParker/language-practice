# Lesson 27 · Saber vs conocer
**Week 5 · Irregular verbs and people** · about 30 min · [[Spanish 8-Week Course|Course overview]] · ← [[26 - Tener, venir and decir|Lesson 26]] · [[28 - Possessives and family|Lesson 28]] →

> [!tip] Goal
> Choose between the two verbs for "to know".

## 1 · Learn (10 min)
Both mean "to know", but they aren't interchangeable.

| saber | conocer |
|---|---|
| facts and information | people, places, things you're familiar with |
| how to do something (+ infinitive) | meeting someone for the first time |
| **sé**, sabes, sabe, sabemos, saben | **conozco**, conoces, conoce, conocemos, conocen |

- **No sé.** = I don't know. · **Sé nadar.** = I know how to swim.
- **Conozco Madrid.** = I know (have been to) Madrid.
- When the thing you know is a person, add **a**: **Conozco a María.** This "personal a" goes before any person who receives the action.

**New words**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| saber | to know (facts, how to) |
| conocer | to know (people, places) |
| nadar | to swim |
| la respuesta | answer |
| el barrio | neighborhood |
| bien | well |
