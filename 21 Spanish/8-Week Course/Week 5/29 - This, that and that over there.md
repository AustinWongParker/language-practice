# Lesson 29 · This, that and that over there
**Week 5 · Irregular verbs and people** · about 30 min · [[Spanish 8-Week Course|Course overview]] · ← [[28 - Possessives and family|Lesson 28]] · [[30 - Week 5 review - family and pets|Lesson 30]] →

> [!tip] Goal
> Point things out with este, ese and aquel.

## 1 · Learn (10 min)
| | this (near me) | that (near you) | that (over there) |
|---|---|---|---|
| masculine | **este** | **ese** | **aquel** |
| feminine | **esta** | **esa** | **aquella** |
| masculine plural | **estos** | **esos** | **aquellos** |
| feminine plural | **estas** | **esas** | **aquellas** |

- **Esto / eso** (no gender) point to an idea or something you can't name: **¿Qué es esto?** · **Eso es todo.** (That's all.)
- **cerca** = near · **lejos** = far

**New words**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| este / esta | this |
| ese / esa | that |
| aquel / aquella | that (over there) |
| esto / eso | this / that (idea) |
| la camisa | shirt |
| el edificio | building |
| cerca | near |
| lejos | far |

## 2 · Practice (10 min)
Say each one out loud in Spanish, then type it after the arrow.

1. This shirt is new. → 
2. I want that book (near you). → 
3. That building over there is very old. → 
4. What is this? → 
5. These questions are difficult. → 

> [!check]- Answers (open after you try)
> 1. Esta camisa es nueva.
> 2. Quiero ese libro.
> 3. Aquel edificio es muy viejo.
> 4. ¿Qué es esto?
> 5. Estas preguntas son difíciles.

## 3 · Listen (10 min)
Watch a **Superbeginner or Beginner** video from [Dreaming Spanish](https://www.youtube.com/@DreamingSpanish) on YouTube. Don't translate in your head; follow along from the pictures and gestures.
**Listen for:** este and ese: what are people pointing at?
