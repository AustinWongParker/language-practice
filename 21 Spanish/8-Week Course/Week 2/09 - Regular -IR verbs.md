# Lesson 9 · Regular -IR verbs
**Week 2 · Present tense** · about 30 min · [[Spanish 8-Week Course|Course overview]] · ← [[08 - Regular -ER verbs|Lesson 8]] · [[10 - Gender, articles and plurals|Lesson 10]] →

> [!tip] Goal
> Use regular -ir verbs, and spot the two forms that differ from -er.

## 1 · Learn (10 min)
**-ir** verbs share almost all of the -er endings. Only **nosotros** and **vosotros** differ:

| | vivir (to live) | ending |
|---|---|---|
| yo | **vivo** | -o |
| tú | **vives** | -es |
| él / ella / usted | **vive** | -e |
| nosotros | **vivimos** | **-imos** |
| vosotros | **vivís** | **-ís** |
| ellos / ustedes | **viven** | -en |

**New words**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| vivir | to live |
| escribir | to write |
| abrir | to open |
| recibir | to receive |
| compartir | to share |
| la ciudad | city |
| el correo | email, mail |
| la tienda | store |
| grande | big |

## 2 · Practice (10 min)
Say each one out loud in Spanish, then type it after the arrow.

1. I live in a big city. → 
2. Where do you live? → 
3. We write emails at work. → 
4. The store opens at nine. → 
5. They share an apartment. → 

> [!check]- Answers (open after you try)
> 1. Vivo en una ciudad grande.
> 2. ¿Dónde vives?
> 3. Escribimos correos en el trabajo.
> 4. La tienda abre a las nueve.
> 5. Comparten un apartamento.

## 3 · Listen (10 min)
Watch a **Superbeginner** video from [Dreaming Spanish](https://www.youtube.com/@DreamingSpanish) on YouTube. Don't translate in your head; follow along from the pictures and gestures.
**Listen for:** vivo, vive, vivimos: where do people live?
