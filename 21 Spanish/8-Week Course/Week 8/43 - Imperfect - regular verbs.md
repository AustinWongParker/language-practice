# Lesson 43 · Imperfect - regular verbs
**Week 8 · Imperfect and wrap-up** · about 30 min · [[Spanish 8-Week Course|Course overview]] · ← [[42 - Week 7 review - what did you do yesterday|Lesson 42]] · [[44 - Imperfect - ser, ir, ver|Lesson 44]] →

> [!tip] Goal
> Describe what used to happen and what was going on.

## 1 · Learn (10 min)
The **imperfect** describes the past as ongoing: what used to happen, what was going on, what things were like.

| | hablar | comer | vivir |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | **hablaba** | **comía** | **vivía** |
| tú | **hablabas** | **comías** | **vivías** |
| él / ella / usted | **hablaba** | **comía** | **vivía** |
| nosotros | **hablábamos** | **comíamos** | **vivíamos** |
| ellos / ustedes | **hablaban** | **comían** | **vivían** |

- English often says "used to" or "was …-ing": **Jugaba al fútbol** = I used to play soccer.
- The **yo** and **él** forms are the same, so add the pronoun if it's unclear.
- **De niño / de niña** = as a kid · **antes** = before, back then

**New words**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| de niño / de niña | as a kid |
| antes | before, back then |
| jugar al fútbol | to play soccer |
| cerca de | near |
| todos los veranos | every summer |
| tenía | I had, used to have |

## 2 · Practice (10 min)
Say each one out loud in Spanish, then type it after the arrow.

1. As a kid, I played soccer. → 
2. We lived near the beach. → 
3. She always ate at home. → 
4. Before, I didn't drink coffee. → 
5. I had a big dog. → 

> [!check]- Answers (open after you try)
> 1. De niño (de niña), jugaba al fútbol.
> 2. Vivíamos cerca de la playa.
> 3. Siempre comía en casa.
> 4. Antes no bebía café.
> 5. Tenía un perro grande.
