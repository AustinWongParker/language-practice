# Lesson 44 · Imperfect - ser, ir, ver
**Week 8 · Imperfect and wrap-up** · about 30 min · [[Spanish 8-Week Course|Course overview]] · ← [[43 - Imperfect - regular verbs|Lesson 43]] · [[45 - Preterite vs imperfect|Lesson 45]] →

> [!tip] Goal
> Use the three irregular imperfect verbs to describe the past.

## 1 · Learn (10 min)
Only three verbs are irregular in the imperfect:

| | ser | ir | ver |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | **era** | **iba** | **veía** |
| tú | **eras** | **ibas** | **veías** |
| él / ella / usted | **era** | **iba** | **veía** |
| nosotros | **éramos** | **íbamos** | **veíamos** |
| ellos / ustedes | **eran** | **iban** | **veían** |

- Describing the past: **Era muy tímido.** · **La casa era pequeña.**
- Time and age in the past: **Eran las diez.** · **Tenía diez años.**
- **Cuando era niño (niña)…** = When I was a kid…

**New words**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| era | was (describing) |
| iba | used to go |
| veía | used to watch, see |
| cuando era niño / niña | when I was a kid |
| la escuela | school |
| los dibujos animados | cartoons |
| tímido / tímida | shy |
| feliz | happy |

## 2 · Practice (10 min)
Say each one out loud in Spanish, then type it after the arrow.

1. When I was a kid, I was very shy. → 
2. We used to go to the beach every summer. → 
3. I used to watch cartoons. → 
4. It was ten o'clock. → 
5. My school was small. → 

> [!check]- Answers (open after you try)

> 2. Íbamos a la playa todos los veranos.
> 3. Veía dibujos animados.
> 4. Eran las diez.
> 5. Mi escuela era pequeña.
