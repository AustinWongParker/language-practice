# Lesson 23 · Sequence and frequency words
**Week 4 · Stem changers and routines** · about 30 min · [[Spanish 8-Week Course|Course overview]] · ← [[22 - Reflexive verbs|Lesson 22]] · [[24 - Week 4 review - describe your morning|Lesson 24]] →

> [!tip] Goal
> Say in what order and how often you do things.

## 1 · Learn (10 min)
**Order of events:** **primero** (first) → **luego / después** (then) → **más tarde** (later) → **por último** (finally)

**Before / after:** **antes de** / **después de** + infinitive: **Después de comer, camino.** = After eating, I walk.

**How often:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **siempre** | always |
| **normalmente** | usually |
| **a veces** | sometimes |
| **casi nunca** | hardly ever |
| **nunca** | never |
| **todos los días** | every day |

**Nunca** can go before the verb (**Nunca bebo café**) or after it with **no** (**No bebo café nunca**).

**New words**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| primero | first |
| luego | then |
| después de | after |
| antes de | before |
| a veces | sometimes |
| nunca | never |
| normalmente | usually |
| por último | finally |

## 2 · Practice (10 min)
Say each one out loud in Spanish, then type it after the arrow.

1. First I get up, then I shower. → 
2. I never drink coffee at night. → 
3. Sometimes we walk after eating. → 
4. Before working, I go to the gym. → 
5. She usually goes to bed at eleven. → 

> [!check]- Answers (open after you try)
> 1. Primero me levanto, luego me ducho.
> 2. Nunca bebo café por la noche.
> 3. A veces caminamos después de comer.
> 4. Antes de trabajar, voy al gimnasio.
> 5. Normalmente se acuesta a las once.

## 3 · Listen (10 min)
Watch a **Superbeginner or Beginner** video from [Dreaming Spanish](https://www.youtube.com/@DreamingSpanish) on YouTube. Don't translate in your head; follow along from the pictures and gestures.
**Listen for:** primero, luego, después: the order things happen in.
