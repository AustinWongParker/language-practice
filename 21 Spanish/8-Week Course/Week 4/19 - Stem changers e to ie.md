# Lesson 19 · Stem changers e to ie
**Week 4 · Stem changers and routines** · about 30 min · [[Spanish 8-Week Course|Course overview]] · ← [[18 - Week 3 review - plan your weekend|Lesson 18]] · [[20 - Stem changers o to ue|Lesson 20]] →

> [!tip] Goal
> Use verbs whose e changes to ie.

## 1 · Learn (10 min)
Some verbs change their stem vowel when it's stressed: **e → ie**. The change happens in every form **except nosotros and vosotros**. Draw a line around the forms that change and you'll see why they're called "boot verbs".

| | pensar (to think) |
|---|---|
| yo | **pienso** |
| tú | **piensas** |
| él / ella / usted | **piensa** |
| nosotros | **pensamos** |
| vosotros | **pensáis** |
| ellos / ustedes | **piensan** |

Same pattern: **querer** (quiero), **empezar** (empiezo), **preferir** (prefiero), **entender** (entiendo), **cerrar** (cierro).

**New words**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| pensar | to think |
| pensar que | to think that |
| empezar | to start |
| preferir | to prefer |
| entender | to understand |
| cerrar | to close |
| la película | movie |

## 2 · Practice (10 min)
Say each one out loud in Spanish, then type it after the arrow.

1. I think it's easy. → 
2. The class starts early. → 
3. Do you prefer coffee or tea? → 
4. We understand the movie. → 
5. They close the store at ten. →

> 2. La clase empieza temprano.
> 3. ¿Prefieres café o té?
> 4. Entendemos la película.
> 5. Cierran la tienda a las diez.

## 3 · Listen (10 min)
Watch a **Superbeginner or Beginner** video from [Dreaming Spanish](https://www.youtube.com/@DreamingSpanish) on YouTube. Don't translate in your head; follow along from the pictures and gestures.
**Listen for:** ie verbs: quiero, pienso, prefiero, empieza.
