# Lesson 5 · Numbers, age and hay
**Week 1 · Foundations** · about 30 min · [[Spanish 8-Week Course|Course overview]] · ← [[04 - Want, can and have to|Lesson 4]] · [[06 - Week 1 review - introduce yourself|Lesson 6]] →

> [!tip] Goal
> Count to 30, say your age, and say what there is.

## 1 · Learn (10 min)
**0–15:** cero, uno, dos, tres, cuatro, cinco, seis, siete, ocho, nueve, diez, once, doce, trece, catorce, quince
**16–19:** dieciséis, diecisiete, dieciocho, diecinueve
**20–29:** veinte, veintiuno, veintidós, veintitrés, veinticuatro, veinticinco, veintiséis, veintisiete, veintiocho, veintinueve
**30:** treinta (31 = treinta y uno, 32 = treinta y dos…)

**Age uses tener** (to have), not ser: **Tengo 30 años** = I'm 30 (literally "I have 30 years"). **¿Cuántos años tienes?** = How old are you?

**Hay** = there is / there are, the same word for one or many: **Hay un perro. Hay tres perros.** · **¿Cuántos libros hay?**

**New words**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| tener … años | to be … years old |
| hay | there is, there are |
| ¿cuántos? / ¿cuántas? | how many? |
| el año | year |
| el perro | dog |
| el libro | book |
| la casa | house |
| la persona | person |

## 3 · Listen (10 min)
Watch a **Superbeginner** video from [Dreaming Spanish](https://www.youtube.com/@DreamingSpanish) on YouTube. Don't translate in your head; follow along from the pictures and gestures.
**Listen for:** numbers; try to catch every one you hear.
