# Lesson 4 · Want, can and have to
**Week 1 · Foundations** · about 30 min · [[Spanish 8-Week Course|Course overview]] · ← [[03 - Estar for feelings and location|Lesson 3]] · [[05 - Numbers, age and hay|Lesson 5]] →

> [!tip] Goal
> Say what you want to do, can do and have to do.

## 1 · Learn (10 min)
Three verbs that take another verb straight after them, in its "to ___" form (the infinitive). You covered these in [[Expressing Desire, Ability and Obligation in Spanish]].

| | querer (want) | poder (can) | tener que (have to) |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | **quiero** | **puedo** | **tengo que** |
| tú | **quieres** | **puedes** | **tienes que** |
| él / ella / usted | **quiere** | **puede** | **tiene que** |
| nosotros | **queremos** | **podemos** | **tenemos que** |
| ellos / ustedes | **quieren** | **pueden** | **tienen que** |

- **Quiero aprender español.** · **¿Puedes venir?** · **Tengo que trabajar.**
- Notice the vowel change (**e → ie**, **o → ue**) everywhere except **nosotros**. You'll meet more verbs like this in week 4.
- Negative: **No puedo salir.**

**New words**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| querer | to want |
| poder | to be able to, can |
| tener que | to have to |
| aprender | to learn |
| practicar | to practice |
| salir | to go out, leave |
| comer | to eat |
| ahora | now |
| esta noche | tonight |

## 2 · Practice (10 min)
Say each one out loud in Spanish, then type it after the arrow.

1. I want to learn Spanish. → 
2. Can you practice today? → 
3. I have to work tomorrow. → 
4. We want to eat now. → 
5. She can't go out tonight. → 

> [!check]- Answers (open after you try)
> 1. Quiero aprender español.
> 2. ¿Puedes practicar hoy?
> 3. Tengo que trabajar mañana.
> 4. Queremos comer ahora.
> 5. Ella no puede salir esta noche.

## 3 · Listen (10 min)
Watch a **Superbeginner** video from [Dreaming Spanish](https://www.youtube.com/@DreamingSpanish) on YouTube. Don't translate in your head; follow along from the pictures and gestures.
**Listen for:** quiero, puedo and tengo que followed by another verb.
