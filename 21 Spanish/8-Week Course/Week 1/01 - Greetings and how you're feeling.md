# Lesson 1 · Greetings and how you're feeling
**Week 1 · Foundations** · about 30 min · [[Spanish 8-Week Course|Course overview]] · [[02 - Ser for who you are and where you're from|Lesson 2]] →

> [!tip] Goal
> Greet people, ask how they are, and say goodbye.

## 1 · Learn (10 min)
**Saying hi**
- **Hola** works any time of day.
- **Buenos días** (morning) · **Buenas tardes** (afternoon) · **Buenas noches** (evening and night)

**How are you?**
- **¿Cómo estás?** to a friend · **¿Cómo está usted?** to someone you'd be formal with
- Answers: **Estoy bien / muy bien / más o menos / cansado** (**cansada** for a woman), then **¿Y tú?**

**Names**
- **Me llamo …** = My name is … · **¿Cómo te llamas?** = What's your name?
- **Mucho gusto** = Nice to meet you

**Goodbye:** **Adiós** · **Hasta luego** (see you later) · **Hasta mañana** (see you tomorrow) · **Nos vemos** (see you)

> [!note] Pronunciation check
> Vowels are always short and pure: a (ah), e (eh), i (ee), o (oh), u (oo). **h** is silent (**hola** sounds like "ola"), **ll** sounds like y, and **ñ** like "ny".

**New words**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| hola | hello |
| buenos días | good morning |
| buenas noches | good evening, good night |
| ¿cómo estás? | how are you? |
| bien | well, fine |
| cansado / cansada | tired |
| mucho gusto | nice to meet you |
| hasta luego | see you later |

## 2 · Practice (10 min)
Say each one out loud in Spanish, then type it after the arrow.

1. Good morning! How are you? → 
2. I'm very well, thanks. And you? → 
3. My name is Austin. What's your name? → 
4. Nice to meet you. → 
5. I'm tired today. See you tomorrow! → 

> [!check]- Answers (open after you try)
> 1. ¡Buenos días! ¿Cómo estás?
> 2. Estoy muy bien, gracias. ¿Y tú?
> 3. Me llamo Austin. ¿Cómo te llamas?
> 4. Mucho gusto.
> 5. Hoy estoy cansado (cansada). ¡Hasta mañana!

## 3 · Listen (10 min)
Watch a **Superbeginner** video from [Dreaming Spanish](https://www.youtube.com/@DreamingSpanish) on YouTube. Don't translate in your head; follow along from the pictures and gestures.
**Listen for:** greetings like hola, buenos días and ¿cómo estás?
