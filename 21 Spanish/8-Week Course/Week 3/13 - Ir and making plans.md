# Lesson 13 · Ir and making plans
**Week 3 · Everyday life** · about 30 min · [[Spanish 8-Week Course|Course overview]] · ← [[12 - Week 2 review - your daily routine|Lesson 12]] · [[14 - Telling time and days of the week|Lesson 14]] →

> [!tip] Goal
> Say where you're going and what you're going to do.

## 1 · Learn (10 min)
**Ir** (to go) is irregular:

| | ir |
|---|---|
| yo | **voy** |
| tú | **vas** |
| él / ella / usted | **va** |
| nosotros | **vamos** |
| vosotros | **vais** |
| ellos / ustedes | **van** |

- Going somewhere: **ir a** + place, and **a + el = al**: **Voy al gimnasio.** · **¿Adónde vas?** = Where are you going?
- Plans: **ir a + infinitive** = going to do something: **Voy a estudiar esta noche.**
- **¡Vamos!** = Let's go!

**New words**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| ir | to go |
| el gimnasio | gym |
| el trabajo | work, job |
| el supermercado | supermarket |
| la playa | beach |
| el fin de semana | weekend |
| mañana | tomorrow |
| hacer | to do, make |

## 2 · Practice (10 min)
Say each one out loud in Spanish, then type it after the arrow.

1. I'm going to the gym. →

3. We're going to cook tonight. → 
4. She's going to the beach this weekend. → 
5. What are you going to do tomorrow? →

> 4. Ella va a la playa este fin de semana.

## 3 · Listen (10 min)
Watch a **Superbeginner** video from [Dreaming Spanish](https://www.youtube.com/@DreamingSpanish) on YouTube. Don't translate in your head; follow along from the pictures and gestures.
**Listen for:** voy a / vamos a: what are people planning?
