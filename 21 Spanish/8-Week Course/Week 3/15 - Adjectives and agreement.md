# Lesson 15 · Adjectives and agreement
**Week 3 · Everyday life** · about 30 min · [[Spanish 8-Week Course|Course overview]] · ← [[14 - Telling time and days of the week|Lesson 14]] · [[16 - Gustar for likes and dislikes|Lesson 16]] →

> [!tip] Goal
> Describe things with adjectives that match their noun.

## 1 · Learn (10 min)
Adjectives usually go **after** the noun and match it in gender and number:

| | one | many |
|---|---|---|
| masculine | un perro **pequeño** | unos perros **pequeños** |
| feminine | una casa **pequeña** | unas casas **pequeñas** |

- Adjectives ending in **-e** or a consonant don't change for gender: **grande, fácil** → plural **grandes, fáciles**.
- **muy** = very: **muy bonito**.
- **bueno / malo** can go before a masculine noun and shorten: **un buen día, un mal día**.

**New words**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| grande | big |
| pequeño / pequeña | small |
| nuevo / nueva | new |
| viejo / vieja | old |
| fácil | easy |
| difícil | hard, difficult |
| bonito / bonita | pretty, nice |
| rápido / rápida | fast |
