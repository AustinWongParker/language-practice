## 2 · Speak and write (15 min)

- where you're going and what you're going to do (**ir a**, lesson 13)
- what time and which day (lesson 14)
- describe the places and people with adjectives (lesson 15)
- what you like and don't like about the plan (lesson 16)
- what you'll order if you eat out (lesson 17)
