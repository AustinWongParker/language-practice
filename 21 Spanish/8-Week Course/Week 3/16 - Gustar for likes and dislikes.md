# Lesson 16 · Gustar for likes and dislikes
**Week 3 · Everyday life** · about 30 min · [[Spanish 8-Week Course|Course overview]] · ← [[15 - Adjectives and agreement|Lesson 15]] · [[17 - Food and ordering|Lesson 17]] →

> [!tip] Goal
> Say what you and others like and don't like.

## 1 · Learn (10 min)
**Gustar** works backwards from English: the thing you like is the subject. **Me gusta el café** = coffee is pleasing to me.

- One thing or an action → **gusta**: **Me gusta el café. Me gusta correr.**
- Several things → **gustan**: **Me gustan los perros.**
- Who likes it: **me** (me) · **te** (you) · **le** (him / her / you formal) · **nos** (us) · **les** (them / you all)
- Negative: **No me gusta.** · Agreeing: **A mí también** (me too) · **A mí tampoco** (me neither)

**New words**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| gustar | to like (be pleasing) |
| el café | coffee |
| el té | tea |
| la música | music |
| la comida | food |
| también | also, too |
| tampoco | neither |
| mucho | a lot |

## 2 · Practice (10 min)
Say each one out loud in Spanish, then type it after the arrow.

1. I like coffee. → 
2. Do you like dogs? → 
3. We like to run. → 
4. She doesn't like the music. →

> [!check]- Answers (open after you try)
> 1. Me gusta el café.
> 2. ¿Te gustan los perros?
> 3. Nos gusta correr.
> 4. No le gusta la música.
> 5. Me gustan mucho los libros.

**Listen for:** me gusta / no me gusta: what do people like?
