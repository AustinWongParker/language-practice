# 📖 My Dictionary

Every word from the [[Spanish 1000 Word List]], A to Z. **Say it like** spells each word the way it sounds in English letters, with the **STRESSED** syllable in capitals. Pronunciation is Latin American (as in the flashcards). Tap a number to open the lesson that teaches the word.

> [!tip] Sound key
> **ah** as in *father* · **eh** as in *bet* · **ee** as in *see* · **oh** as in *go* · **oo** as in *food*  
> **r** is a quick tap (like the *tt* in American *butter*) · **rr** is rolled · **h** sounds like the *h* in *hello* (Spanish *j*, and *g* before e/i) · Spanish **h** is always silent
