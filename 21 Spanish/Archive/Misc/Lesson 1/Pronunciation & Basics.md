5 vowels in Spanish (12 in english)

a - ah
e - eh
i - ee
o - oh
u - oo

c has a hard 'c' sound as in cuh
- sounds like K except before the letters E or I then it sounds like S
	- criminal
	- social
g has a hard 'g' sound like guh
- Hard G except before an E or I then it sounds like an English H
	- Legal 
	- Digital 
V In Spanish sounds like a soft B, like in boy and Ben

H is silent
- Hospital

j sounds like an H in English
- Judicial

Masculine vs feminine 
- Masculine tend to end in an O, but not always
	- chico - boy
	- hombre - man
	- parque - park
		- qu --> 'k' sound
	- edificio - building
- feminine tend to end in a sound, but not always
	- chica - girl
	- mujer - woman
	- casa - house
	- silla - chair
		- double ll --> 'y' sound 

Definite article in English is 'THE'
Indefinite article in English is A or AN
In Spanish, the definite and indefinite articles agree in number and gender with the noun
- Masculine Nouns
	- el - the (singular)
	- un - a, an (singular)
	- los - the (plural)
	- unos - some
	- el chico - the boy
	- ----
	- árbol - tree
	- el árbol - the tree
	- un árbol - a tree
	- los árboles - the trees
	- unos árboles - some trees
- Feminine Nouns
	- la - the (singular)
	- una - a, an (singular)
	- las - the (plural)
	- unas - some
	- ---
	- la chica - the girl
	- una chica - a girl
	- las chicas - the girls
	- unas chicas - some girls
	- ---
	- mujer - woman
	- la mujer - the woman
	- una mujer - a woman 
	- las mujeres - the women
	- unas mujeres - some women

[[Verbs|Verbs in Spanish]]
- If it can be ended in ación, it can be turned into a verb
- Drop the ación, add r
- ~~~
- informar - to inform
- confirmar - to confirm
- implementar - to implement
- motivar - to motivate
- certificar - to certify

3 types of verbs:
- AR
- ER
	- comer
		- to eat
- IR
	- vivir
		- to live
