## 2 · Speak and write (15 min)
Write 6–8 sentences introducing yourself, using something from every lesson this week:
- a greeting and your name (lesson 1)
- where you're from and what you do (lesson 2)
- how you are today and where you are right now (lesson 3)
- something you want to do, can do and have to do this week (lesson 4)
- your age, or how many of something there are in your home (lesson 5)

Then read it out loud and record it with your phone's voice memo app. **Keep this recording**: you'll compare it with your final one in lesson 48.
