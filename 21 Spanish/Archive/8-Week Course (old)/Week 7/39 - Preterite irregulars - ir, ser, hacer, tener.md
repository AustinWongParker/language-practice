# Lesson 39 · Preterite irregulars - ir, ser, hacer, tener
**Week 7 · The past (preterite)** · about 30 min · [[Spanish 8-Week Course|Course overview]] · ← [[38 - Preterite - regular -ER and -IR verbs|Lesson 38]] · [[40 - Preterite irregulars - estar, poder, decir, ver, dar|Lesson 40]] →

> [!tip] Goal
> Use the most common irregular past-tense verbs.

## 1 · Learn (10 min)
The most common verbs are irregular in the preterite, and these forms have **no accents**.

| | ir / ser | hacer | tener |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | **fui** | **hice** | **tuve** |
| tú | **fuiste** | **hiciste** | **tuviste** |
| él / ella / usted | **fue** | **hizo** | **tuvo** |
| nosotros | **fuimos** | **hicimos** | **tuvimos** |
| ellos / ustedes | **fueron** | **hicieron** | **tuvieron** |

- **Ir** and **ser** are identical in the preterite: **Fui al gimnasio** (I went) vs **Fue divertido** (it was fun). Context decides.
- **¿Qué hiciste?** = What did you do?

**New words**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| fui | I went, I was |
| hice | I did, made |
| tuve | I had |
| el viaje | trip |
| el examen | exam |
| divertido / divertida | fun |
| pasado / pasada | last (week, weekend) |
