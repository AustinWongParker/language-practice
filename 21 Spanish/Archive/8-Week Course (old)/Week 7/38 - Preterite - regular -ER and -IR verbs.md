# Lesson 38 · Preterite - regular -ER and -IR verbs
**Week 7 · The past (preterite)** · about 30 min · [[Spanish 8-Week Course|Course overview]] · ← [[37 - Preterite - regular -AR verbs|Lesson 37]] · [[39 - Preterite irregulars - ir, ser, hacer, tener|Lesson 39]] →

> [!tip] Goal
> Say what you did with regular -er and -ir verbs.

## 1 · Learn (10 min)
**-er** and **-ir** verbs share one set of preterite endings:

| | comer | vivir | ending |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | **comí** | **viví** | -í |
| tú | **comiste** | **viviste** | -iste |
| él / ella / usted | **comió** | **vivió** | -ió |
| nosotros | **comimos** | **vivimos** | -imos |
| vosotros | **comisteis** | **vivisteis** | -isteis |
| ellos / ustedes | **comieron** | **vivieron** | -ieron |

- **leer** and **creer** use a **y**: **leyó, leyeron** · **creyó, creyeron**.
- -ir stem changers change in the él and ellos forms: **pidió, pidieron** · **durmió, durmieron**.

**New words**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| salí | I went out |
| escribí | I wrote |
| aprendí | I learned |
| volví | I came back |
| leyó | he / she read |
| el mensaje | message |
| la carta | letter |
