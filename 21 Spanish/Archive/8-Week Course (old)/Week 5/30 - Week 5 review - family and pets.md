## 2 · Speak and write (15 min)
Describe your family and pets in 8–10 sentences:
- who they are and what they're like (**ser**, adjectives)
- where they live and what they do (present tense)
- something they always do or say (**hacer**, **decir**, **tener** expressions)
- Cooper: what Cooper likes and where you walk together (**gustar**, possessives)
