# 🇪🇸 Spanish 8-Week Course

> [!warning] Archived
> Replaced by the [[Spanish 1000 Course]], which includes all of these lessons. Kept for reference; the lesson tracker ignores this folder.

An early-beginner course at about 30 minutes a day. It builds on what's already in your notes (pronunciation, greetings, querer / poder / tener que) and ends with the past tenses and a two-minute monologue.

## How it works
- Your next lesson appears in Bookmarks under **🎯 Today's Goals**, and in a pop-up when Obsidian opens.
- Each lesson has three parts: **Learn** (10 min), **Practice** (10 min) and **Listen** (10 min), then a checklist.
- Check every box and the lesson is done: it records today's date and clears from Today's Goals. The next lesson appears tomorrow.
- Missed a day or a week? Nothing is lost. The same lesson waits for you.
- Before the last box, fill in `minutes`, `difficulty` (1 = easy, 5 = hard) and `reflection` at the top of the lesson. They add up on [[Progress]].
- To pause the course, set `active` above to `false`. Set it back to `true` to resume.
- To redo a lesson, uncheck any box and it goes back to "todo".

There are 48 lessons: 6 a week for 8 weeks, so one day a week is free. Every sixth lesson is a review.
