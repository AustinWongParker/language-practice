# Lesson 7 · Regular -AR verbs
**Week 2 · Present tense** · about 30 min · [[Spanish 8-Week Course|Course overview]] · ← [[06 - Week 1 review - introduce yourself|Lesson 6]] · [[08 - Regular -ER verbs|Lesson 8]] →

> [!tip] Goal
> Say what you do every day with regular -ar verbs.

## 1 · Learn (10 min)
Most verbs end in **-ar**. Drop the -ar and add the ending for who's doing it:

| | hablar (to speak) | ending |
|---|---|---|
| yo | **hablo** | -o |
| tú | **hablas** | -as |
| él / ella / usted | **habla** | -a |
| nosotros | **hablamos** | -amos |
| vosotros | **habláis** | -áis |
| ellos / ustedes | **hablan** | -an |

- One form covers three English ones: **hablo** = I speak / I'm speaking / I do speak.
- **a + el** joins into **al**: **Paseo al perro** = I walk the dog.

**New words**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| hablar | to speak |
| trabajar | to work |
| estudiar | to study |
| caminar | to walk |
| pasear al perro | to walk the dog |
| cocinar | to cook |
| necesitar | to need |
| todos los días | every day |
| la cena | dinner |

## 3 · Listen (10 min)
Watch a **Superbeginner** video from [Dreaming Spanish](https://www.youtube.com/@DreamingSpanish) on YouTube. Don't translate in your head; follow along from the pictures and gestures.
**Listen for:** -o and -amos endings: who is doing the action?
