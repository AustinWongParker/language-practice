# Lesson 11 · Question words
**Week 2 · Present tense** · about 30 min · [[Spanish 8-Week Course|Course overview]] · ← [[10 - Gender, articles and plurals|Lesson 10]] · [[12 - Week 2 review - your daily routine|Lesson 12]] →

> [!tip] Goal
> Ask what, who, where, when, why, how and how many.

## 1 · Learn (10 min)
| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **¿Qué?** | What? |
| **¿Quién? / ¿Quiénes?** | Who? (one / more than one) |
| **¿Dónde?** | Where? |
| **¿Cuándo?** | When? |
| **¿Por qué?** | Why? (answer with **porque** = because) |
| **¿Cómo?** | How? |
| **¿Cuánto? / ¿Cuánta? / ¿Cuántos? / ¿Cuántas?** | How much? / How many? |
| **¿Cuál?** | Which? |

- Question words always carry an accent.
- Word order: question word + verb + subject: **¿Dónde vive tu hermano?**
- Written questions open with **¿** and exclamations with **¡**.

**New words**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| porque | because |
| con | with |
| mi / mis | my |
| tu / tus | your |
| el hermano | brother |
| la hermana | sister |
