# Lesson 8 · Regular -ER verbs
**Week 2 · Present tense** · about 30 min · [[Spanish 8-Week Course|Course overview]] · ← [[07 - Regular -AR verbs|Lesson 7]] · [[09 - Regular -IR verbs|Lesson 9]] →

> [!tip] Goal
> Use regular -er verbs in the present tense.

## 1 · Learn (10 min)
**-er** verbs work the same way with slightly different endings:

| | comer (to eat) | ending |
|---|---|---|
| yo | **como** | -o |
| tú | **comes** | -es |
| él / ella / usted | **come** | -e |
| nosotros | **comemos** | -emos |
| vosotros | **coméis** | -éis |
| ellos / ustedes | **comen** | -en |

Tip: -ar verbs use **a** in their endings, -er verbs use **e**: **hablas** vs **comes**.

**New words**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| comer | to eat |
| beber | to drink |
| leer | to read |
| aprender | to learn |

## 3 · Listen (10 min)
Watch a **Superbeginner** video from [Dreaming Spanish](https://www.youtube.com/@DreamingSpanish) on YouTube. Don't translate in your head; follow along from the pictures and gestures.
**Listen for:** -es and -e endings (comes, come).
