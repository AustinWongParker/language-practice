# Lesson 14 · Telling time and days of the week
**Week 3 · Everyday life** · about 30 min · [[Spanish 8-Week Course|Course overview]] · ← [[13 - Ir and making plans|Lesson 13]] · [[15 - Adjectives and agreement|Lesson 15]] →

> [!tip] Goal
> Tell the time and say when things happen.

## 1 · Learn (10 min)
**What time is it?** **¿Qué hora es?**
- 1:00 **Es la una.** · 2:00 **Son las dos.** (plural for every hour after one)
- :15 **y cuarto** · :30 **y media** · :45 **menos cuarto** (quarter to the next hour)
- 7:30 **Son las siete y media.** · 9:45 **Son las diez menos cuarto.**
- **At** what time? **¿A qué hora…?** → **A las ocho.**

**Days** (lowercase in Spanish): lunes, martes, miércoles, jueves, viernes, sábado, domingo
- **el lunes** = on Monday (this one) · **los lunes** = on Mondays (every week)
- **por la mañana / tarde / noche** = in the morning / afternoon / evening

**New words**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| la hora | hour, time |
| y media | half past |
| y cuarto | quarter past |
| menos cuarto | quarter to |
| hoy | today |
| la semana | week |
| la clase | class |
| temprano | early |

## 2 · Practice (10 min)
Say each one out loud in Spanish, then type it after the arrow.

1. It's one o'clock. → 
2. It's 7:30. → 
3. What time do you work? → 
4. I go to the gym on Mondays and Thursdays. → 
5. The class is at quarter to ten. → 

> [!check]- Answers (open after you try)
> 1. Es la una.
> 2. Son las siete y media.
> 3. ¿A qué hora trabajas?
> 4. Voy al gimnasio los lunes y los jueves.
> 5. La clase es a las diez menos cuarto.
