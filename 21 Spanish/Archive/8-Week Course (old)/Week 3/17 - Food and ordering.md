# Lesson 17 · Food and ordering
**Week 3 · Everyday life** · about 30 min · [[Spanish 8-Week Course|Course overview]] · ← [[16 - Gustar for likes and dislikes|Lesson 16]] · [[18 - Week 3 review - plan your weekend|Lesson 18]] →

> [!tip] Goal
> Talk about meals and order at a restaurant.

## 1 · Learn (10 min)
**Meals:** **el desayuno** (breakfast) · **el almuerzo** (lunch) · **la cena** (dinner). Verbs: **desayunar, almorzar, cenar**.

**At a restaurant**
- **Quisiera…** = I'd like… (polite) · **Para mí…** = For me…
- **¿Me trae…, por favor?** = Could you bring me…?
- **¿Qué me recomienda?** = What do you recommend?
- **La cuenta, por favor.** = The check, please.

**New words**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| el pollo | chicken |
| el arroz | rice |
| la ensalada | salad |
| la carne | meat |
| el pescado | fish |
| quisiera | I'd like |
| la cuenta | the check |
| cenar | to have dinner |

## 2 · Practice (10 min)
Say each one out loud in Spanish, then type it after the arrow.

1. I'd like chicken with rice, please. → 
2. For me, a salad. → 
3. The check, please. → 
4. We have dinner at eight. → 
5. Do you eat meat or fish? →

> 2. Para mí, una ensalada.
> 3. La cuenta, por favor.
> 4. Cenamos a las ocho.
> 5. ¿Comes carne o pescado?

## 3 · Listen (10 min)
Watch a **Superbeginner** video from [Dreaming Spanish](https://www.youtube.com/@DreamingSpanish) on YouTube. Don't translate in your head; follow along from the pictures and gestures.
**Listen for:** food words; make a mental shopping list.
