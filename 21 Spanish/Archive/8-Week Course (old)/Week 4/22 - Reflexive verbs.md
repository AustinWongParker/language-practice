# Lesson 22 · Reflexive verbs
**Week 4 · Stem changers and routines** · about 30 min · [[Spanish 8-Week Course|Course overview]] · ← [[21 - Stem changers e to i and jugar|Lesson 21]] · [[23 - Sequence and frequency words|Lesson 23]] →

> [!tip] Goal
> Describe your routine with reflexive verbs.

## 1 · Learn (10 min)
Reflexive verbs are actions you do to yourself. They end in **-se** and put a pronoun before the verb:

| | levantarse (to get up) |
|---|---|
| yo | **me** levanto |
| tú | **te** levantas |
| él / ella / usted | **se** levanta |
| nosotros | **nos** levantamos |
| vosotros | **os** levantáis |
| ellos / ustedes | **se** levantan |

- With an infinitive, the pronoun can stick to the end: **Tengo que levantarme temprano.**
- Many are stem changers too: **despertarse** (me despierto), **acostarse** (me acuesto), **vestirse** (me visto).
- You already know one: **Me llamo…** (llamarse).

**New words**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| levantarse | to get up |
| despertarse | to wake up |
| ducharse | to shower |
| vestirse | to get dressed |
| acostarse | to go to bed |

## 2 · Practice (10 min)
Say each one out loud in Spanish, then type it after the arrow.

1. I get up at six. → 
2. What time do you go to bed? →

4. We get dressed quickly. → 
5. I have to wake up early. →

> 5. Tengo que despertarme temprano.
