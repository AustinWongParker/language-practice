# Lesson 21 · Stem changers e to i and jugar
**Week 4 · Stem changers and routines** · about 30 min · [[Spanish 8-Week Course|Course overview]] · ← [[20 - Stem changers o to ue|Lesson 20]] · [[22 - Reflexive verbs|Lesson 22]] →

> [!tip] Goal
> Use e-to-i verbs and talk about playing sports.

## 1 · Learn (10 min)
**e → i** happens only in -ir verbs, in the same boot shape:

| | pedir (to ask for, order) |
|---|---|
| yo | **pido** |
| tú | **pides** |
| él / ella / usted | **pide** |
| nosotros | **pedimos** |
| vosotros | **pedís** |
| ellos / ustedes | **piden** |

Same pattern: **servir** (sirvo), **repetir** (repito), **seguir** (sigo; note the spelling).

**Jugar** is the only **u → ue** verb: **juego, juegas, juega, jugamos, jugáis, juegan**. Sports take **a**: **Juego al pickleball.**

**New words**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| pedir | to ask for, order |
| servir | to serve |
| repetir | to repeat |
| seguir | to follow, keep going |
| jugar | to play (games, sports) |
| siempre | always |
| el restaurante | restaurant |
| el plan | plan |

## 2 · Practice (10 min)
Say each one out loud in Spanish, then type it after the arrow.

1. I always order coffee. → 
2. Can you repeat the question? → 
3. We play pickleball on Saturdays. → 
4. The restaurant serves fish. → 
5. She follows the plan. → 

> [!check]- Answers (open after you try)
> 1. Siempre pido café.
> 2. ¿Puedes repetir la pregunta?
> 3. Jugamos al pickleball los sábados.
> 4. El restaurante sirve pescado.
> 5. Ella sigue el plan.

## 3 · Listen (10 min)
Watch a **Superbeginner or Beginner** video from [Dreaming Spanish](https://www.youtube.com/@DreamingSpanish) on YouTube. Don't translate in your head; follow along from the pictures and gestures.
**Listen for:** pide, sirve, juega.
