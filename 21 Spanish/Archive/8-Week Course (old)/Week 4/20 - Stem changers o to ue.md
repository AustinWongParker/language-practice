# Lesson 20 · Stem changers o to ue
**Week 4 · Stem changers and routines** · about 30 min · [[Spanish 8-Week Course|Course overview]] · ← [[19 - Stem changers e to ie|Lesson 19]] · [[21 - Stem changers e to i and jugar|Lesson 21]] →

> [!tip] Goal
> Use verbs whose o changes to ue.

## 1 · Learn (10 min)
Another boot pattern: **o → ue** (again, not in nosotros or vosotros).

| | dormir (to sleep) |
|---|---|
| yo | **duermo** |
| tú | **duermes** |
| él / ella / usted | **duerme** |
| nosotros | **dormimos** |
| vosotros | **dormís** |
| ellos / ustedes | **duermen** |

Same pattern: **poder** (puedo), **volver** (vuelvo), **encontrar** (encuentro), **costar** (cuesta), **almorzar** (almuerzo), **recordar** (recuerdo).

**New words**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| dormir | to sleep |
| volver | to come back |
| encontrar | to find |
| costar | to cost |
| almorzar | to have lunch |
| recordar | to remember |
| las llaves | keys |
| el mediodía | noon |

## 2 · Practice (10 min)
Say each one out loud in Spanish, then type it after the arrow.

1. I sleep eight hours. → 
2. When do you come back home? → 
3. How much does it cost? → 
4. I can't find my keys. → 
5. We have lunch at noon. →

> 1. Duermo ocho horas.
> 2. ¿Cuándo vuelves a casa?
> 3. ¿Cuánto cuesta?
> 4. No encuentro mis llaves.
> 5. Almorzamos al mediodía.

## 3 · Listen (10 min)
Watch a **Superbeginner or Beginner** video from [Dreaming Spanish](https://www.youtube.com/@DreamingSpanish) on YouTube. Don't translate in your head; follow along from the pictures and gestures.
**Listen for:** ue verbs: puedo, vuelvo, duerme, cuesta.
