# Lesson 35 · Weather and seasons
**Week 6 · Pronouns and conversation** · about 30 min · [[Spanish 8-Week Course|Course overview]] · ← [[34 - Comparisons|Lesson 34]] · [[36 - Week 6 review - conversation practice|Lesson 36]] →

> [!tip] Goal
> Talk about the weather and the seasons.

## 1 · Learn (10 min)
**¿Qué tiempo hace?** = What's the weather like?

- **hace** + noun: **hace calor** (hot) · **hace frío** (cold) · **hace sol** (sunny) · **hace viento** (windy) · **hace buen / mal tiempo** (nice / bad weather)
- **llueve** = it rains, it's raining · **nieva** = it snows · **está nublado** = it's cloudy
- Add **mucho**: **hace mucho calor** = it's very hot

**Seasons:** la primavera (spring) · el verano (summer) · el otoño (fall) · el invierno (winter)

Don't mix these up with **tener calor / frío** from lesson 26: **hace frío** is the weather, **tengo frío** is how you feel.

**New words**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| el tiempo | weather |
| hace calor | it's hot |
| hace frío | it's cold |
| hace sol | it's sunny |
| llueve | it's raining |
| nieva | it's snowing |
| el verano | summer |
| el invierno | winter |
