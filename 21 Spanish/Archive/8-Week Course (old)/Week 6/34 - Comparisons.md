# Lesson 34 · Comparisons
**Week 6 · Pronouns and conversation** · about 30 min · [[Spanish 8-Week Course|Course overview]] · ← [[33 - Present progressive (-ing)|Lesson 33]] · [[35 - Weather and seasons|Lesson 35]] →

> [!tip] Goal
> Compare people and things.

## 1 · Learn (10 min)
- **más + adjective + que** = more … than: **más alto que** (taller than)
- **menos + adjective + que** = less … than
- **tan + adjective + como** = as … as: **tan rápido como**
- **tanto / tanta / tantos / tantas + noun + como** = as much / as many … as
- Irregular: **mejor** (better) · **peor** (worse) · **mayor** (older) · **menor** (younger)
- The most: **el / la más …**: **el más rápido** (the fastest)
- After **que** and **como**, use **yo / tú**: **Es mayor que yo.**

## 2 · Practice (10 min)
Say each one out loud in Spanish, then type it after the arrow.

1. Spanish is easier than Chinese. → 
2. My brother is older than me. → 
3. This coffee is better. → 
4. I'm as tired as you. → 
5. He is the fastest. → 

> [!check]- Answers (open after you try)
> 1. El español es más fácil que el chino.
> 2. Mi hermano es mayor que yo.
> 3. Este café es mejor.
> 4. Estoy tan cansado (cansada) como tú.
> 5. Él es el más rápido.

**Listen for:** más … que and mejor or peor.
