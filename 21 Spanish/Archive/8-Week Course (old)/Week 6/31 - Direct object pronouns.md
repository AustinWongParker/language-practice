# Lesson 31 · Direct object pronouns
**Week 6 · Pronouns and conversation** · about 30 min · [[Spanish 8-Week Course|Course overview]] · ← [[30 - Week 5 review - family and pets|Lesson 30]] · [[32 - Indirect objects and gustar-like verbs|Lesson 32]] →

> [!tip] Goal
> Replace a repeated noun with lo, la, los or las.

## 1 · Learn (10 min)
A direct object receives the action (I see **the book**). Replace it with a pronoun to avoid repeating it:

| pronoun | means |
|---|---|
| **me** | me |
| **te** | you |
| **lo** | him, it (masculine), you (formal, man) |
| **la** | her, it (feminine), you (formal, woman) |
| **nos** | us |
| **los / las** | them (masculine / feminine) |

- The pronoun goes **before** a conjugated verb: **¿El libro? Lo veo.**
- With an infinitive you can also attach it: **Lo quiero comprar = Quiero comprarlo.**

**New words**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| ver | to see |
| comprar | to buy |
| llamar | to call |
| buscar | to look for |
| ayudar | to help |
| el regalo | gift |

Watch a **Superbeginner or Beginner** video from [Dreaming Spanish](https://www.youtube.com/@DreamingSpanish) on YouTube. Don't translate in your head; follow along from the pictures and gestures.
**Listen for:** lo and la before verbs.
