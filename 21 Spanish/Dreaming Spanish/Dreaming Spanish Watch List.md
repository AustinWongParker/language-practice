# 🎬 Dreaming Spanish Watch List

All 1018 free Spanish videos on [Dreaming Spanish](https://www.dreaming.com/spanish), from easiest to hardest. The order comes from Dreaming Spanish's own difficulty scores. Click a title to open the video. You need a free Dreaming account to watch, and it logs your hours for you.

How to use it:
- Work down the list and check off each video you finish. When a video feels easy, skip ahead. When it feels like noise, drop back a few.
- 📘 marks the video linked in that lesson of the [[Spanish 1000 Course]]. The course covers the first 120 videos (about 16 hours); after that, just keep going down the list.
- Want more at your level? Dreaming Premium unlocks about 7,400 more videos, and the site's **Library** filters by level.
