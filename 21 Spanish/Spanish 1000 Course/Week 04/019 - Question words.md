## 2 · Learn (8 min)
| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **¿Qué?** | What? |
| **¿Quién? / ¿Quiénes?** | Who? (one / more than one) |
| **¿Dónde?** | Where? |
| **¿Cuándo?** | When? |
| **¿Por qué?** | Why? (answer with **porque** = because) |
| **¿Cómo?** | How? |
| **¿Cuánto? / ¿Cuánta? / ¿Cuántos? / ¿Cuántas?** | How much? / How many? |
| **¿Cuál?** | Which? |

- Question words always carry an accent.
- Word order: question word + verb + subject: **¿Dónde vive tu hermano?**
- Written questions open with **¿** and exclamations with **¡**.

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. Who lives here?
2. Why are you studying Spanish?
3. Because I want to talk with my friends.
4. When do you work?
5. How much water do you drink?

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. ¿Quién vive aquí?
> 2. ¿Por qué estudias español?
> 3. Porque quiero hablar con mis amigos.
> 4. ¿Cuándo trabajas?
> 5. ¿Cuánta agua bebes?

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿Cómo es tu ciudad? ¿Dónde está?** _(What's your city like? Where is it?)_
- **¿Cuándo es tu cumpleaños? ¿Quién es tu mejor amigo?** _(When is your birthday? Who's your best friend?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.

Afterwards, add the best correction you got to [[Spanish Mistakes Log]].

> [!info]- No partner today?
> Answer the questions out loud anyway and record yourself on your phone. Listen back once and note one thing to fix. Or book a session with a tutor (italki, Preply) or a language exchange partner (Tandem, HelloTalk).
