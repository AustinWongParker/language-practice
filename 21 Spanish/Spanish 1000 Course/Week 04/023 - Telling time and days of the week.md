## 2 · Learn (8 min)
**What time is it?** **¿Qué hora es?**
- 1:00 **Es la una.** · 2:00 **Son las dos.** (plural for every hour after one)
- :15 **y cuarto** · :30 **y media** · :45 **menos cuarto** (quarter to the next hour)
- 7:30 **Son las siete y media.** · 9:45 **Son las diez menos cuarto.**
- **At** what time? **¿A qué hora…?** → **A las ocho.**

**Days** (lowercase in Spanish): lunes, martes, miércoles, jueves, viernes, sábado, domingo
- **el lunes** = on Monday (this one) · **los lunes** = on Mondays (every week)
- **por la mañana / tarde / noche** = in the morning / afternoon / evening

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. It's one o'clock.
2. It's 7:30.
3. What time do you work?
4. I go to the gym on Mondays and Thursdays.
5. The class is at quarter to ten.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Es la una.
> 2. Son las siete y media.
> 3. ¿A qué hora trabajas?
> 4. Voy al gimnasio los lunes y los jueves.
> 5. La clase es a las diez menos cuarto.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿Qué día es hoy? ¿Qué haces los lunes?** _(What day is it today? What do you do on Mondays?)_
- **¿A qué hora te levantas y a qué hora trabajas?** _(What time do you get up and what time do you work?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.

Afterwards, add the best correction you got to [[Spanish Mistakes Log]].
