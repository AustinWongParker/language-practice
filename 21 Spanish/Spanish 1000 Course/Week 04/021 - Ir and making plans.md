## 2 · Learn (8 min)
**Ir** (to go) is irregular:

| | ir |
|---|---|
| yo | **voy** |
| tú | **vas** |
| él / ella / usted | **va** |
| nosotros | **vamos** |
| vosotros | **vais** |
| ellos / ustedes | **van** |

- Going somewhere: **ir a** + place, and **a + el = al**: **Voy al gimnasio.** · **¿Adónde vas?** = Where are you going?
- Plans: **ir a + infinitive** = going to do something: **Voy a estudiar esta noche.**
- **¡Vamos!** = Let's go!

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. I'm going to the gym.
2. Where are you going?
3. We're going to cook tonight.
4. She's going to the beach this weekend.
5. What are you going to do tomorrow?

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Voy al gimnasio.
> 2. ¿Adónde vas?
> 3. Vamos a cocinar esta noche.
> 4. Ella va a la playa este fin de semana.
> 5. ¿Qué vas a hacer mañana?

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿Qué vas a hacer el sábado?** _(What are you going to do on Saturday?)_
- **¿Adónde quieres viajar? ¿Por qué?** _(Where do you want to travel? Why?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.
