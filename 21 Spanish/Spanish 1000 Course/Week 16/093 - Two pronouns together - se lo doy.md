## 2 · Learn (8 min)
When there's an indirect and a direct object pronoun together, the **indirect one comes first**: **me lo, te la, nos los**.

- **le** or **les** + **lo / la / los / las** turns into **se**: ~~le lo doy~~ → **Se lo doy.** = I give it to him/her/you.
- Both go before a conjugated verb, or both stick to the end of an infinitive or command (add an accent to keep the stress): **Voy a dártelo.** · **¡Dímelo!**
- Common verbs: **dar, decir, mandar, prestar, explicar, entregar, traer, comprar**.

| Long version | Short version |
|---|---|
| Le doy el libro a Ana. | **Se lo** doy. |
| Me prestas la bici. | **Me la** prestas. |
| Te mando las fotos. | **Te las** mando. |

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. I'll send it to you tomorrow (the message).
2. Can you lend it to me? (the book)
3. She gave them to him (the keys).
4. Tell me!
5. I'm going to explain it to you.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Te lo mando mañana.
> 2. ¿Me lo prestas?
> 3. Se las dio.
> 4. ¡Dímelo!
> 5. Voy a explicártelo.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿A quién le prestas tus cosas?** _(Who do you lend your things to?)_
- **¿Quién te mandó un mensaje hoy?** _(Who sent you a message today?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.
