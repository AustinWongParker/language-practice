## 2 · Learn (8 min)
**Order of events:** **primero** (first) → **luego / después** (then) → **más tarde** (later) → **por último** (finally)

**Before / after:** **antes de** / **después de** + infinitive: **Después de comer, camino.** = After eating, I walk.

**How often:**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **siempre** | always |
| **normalmente** | usually |
| **a veces** | sometimes |
| **casi nunca** | hardly ever |
| **nunca** | never |
| **todos los días** | every day |

**Nunca** can go before the verb (**Nunca bebo café**) or after it with **no** (**No bebo café nunca**).

**Topic words** (used in this grammar point; the frequency words come next)

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| primero | first |
| luego | then |
| después de | after |
| antes de | before |

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. First I get up, then I shower.
2. I never drink coffee at night.
3. Sometimes we walk after eating.
4. Before working, I go to the gym.
5. She usually goes to bed at eleven.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Primero me levanto, luego me ducho.
> 2. Nunca bebo café por la noche.
> 3. A veces caminamos después de comer.
> 4. Antes de trabajar, voy al gimnasio.
> 5. Normalmente se acuesta a las once.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿Qué haces primero por la mañana? ¿Y luego?** _(What do you do first in the morning? And then?)_
- **¿Qué buscas en un trabajo?** _(What do you look for in a job?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.

Afterwards, add the best correction you got to [[Spanish Mistakes Log]].

> Answer the questions out loud anyway and record yourself on your phone. Listen back once and note one thing to fix. Or book a session with a tutor (italki, Preply) or a language exchange partner (Tandem, HelloTalk).
