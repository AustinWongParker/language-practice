## 2 · Learn (8 min)
These verbs are regular except in the **yo** form, which ends in **-go**:

| infinitive | yo | tú | él / ella | nosotros | ellos |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| hacer (do, make) | **hago** | haces | hace | hacemos | hacen |
| poner (put) | **pongo** | pones | pone | ponemos | ponen |
| salir (leave, go out) | **salgo** | sales | sale | salimos | salen |
| traer (bring) | **traigo** | traes | trae | traemos | traen |

- **hacer ejercicio** = to exercise · **poner la mesa** = to set the table · **salir de** = to leave (a place)
- **¿Qué haces?** = What are you doing? / What do you do?

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. I exercise every day.
2. I leave work at five.
3. I'm bringing food to the party.
4. What are you doing?
5. I set the table.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Hago ejercicio todos los días.
> 2. Salgo del trabajo a las cinco.
> 3. Traigo comida a la fiesta.
> 4. ¿Qué haces?
> 5. Pongo la mesa.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿A qué hora sales de casa? ¿Qué traes al trabajo?** _(What time do you leave home? What do you bring to work?)_
- **¿Qué haces cuando tienes miedo?** _(What do you do when you're scared?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.

Afterwards, add the best correction you got to [[Spanish Mistakes Log]].
