## 2 · Learn (8 min)
**-er** verbs work the same way with slightly different endings:

| | comer (to eat) | ending |
|---|---|---|
| yo | **como** | -o |
| tú | **comes** | -es |
| él / ella / usted | **come** | -e |
| nosotros | **comemos** | -emos |
| vosotros | **coméis** | -éis |
| ellos / ustedes | **comen** | -en |

Tip: -ar verbs use **a** in their endings, -er verbs use **e**: **hablas** vs **comes**.

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. I drink water every morning.
2. What are you reading?
3. We're learning Spanish.
4. He runs in the park.
5. They don't understand.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Bebo agua todas las mañanas.
> 2. ¿Qué lees?
> 3. Aprendemos español.
> 4. Él corre en el parque.
> 5. No comprenden.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿Qué comes normalmente en el desayuno?** _(What do you normally eat for breakfast?)_
- **¿Qué libro lees ahora? ¿Qué quieres aprender?** _(What book are you reading? What do you want to learn?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.

Afterwards, add the best correction you got to [[Spanish Mistakes Log]].
