## 2 · Learn (8 min)
**-ir** verbs share almost all of the -er endings. Only **nosotros** and **vosotros** differ:

| | vivir (to live) | ending |
|---|---|---|
| yo | **vivo** | -o |
| tú | **vives** | -es |
| él / ella / usted | **vive** | -e |
| nosotros | **vivimos** | **-imos** |
| vosotros | **vivís** | **-ís** |
| ellos / ustedes | **viven** | -en |

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. I live in a big city.
2. Where do you live?
3. We write emails at work.
4. The store opens at nine.
5. They share an apartment.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Vivo en una ciudad grande.
> 2. ¿Dónde vives?
> 3. Escribimos correos en el trabajo.
> 4. La tienda abre a las nueve.
> 5. Comparten un apartamento.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿Dónde vives? ¿Te gusta vivir ahí?** _(Where do you live? Do you like living there?)_
- **¿Escribes mensajes en español?** _(Do you write messages in Spanish?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.

Afterwards, add the best correction you got to [[Spanish Mistakes Log]].
