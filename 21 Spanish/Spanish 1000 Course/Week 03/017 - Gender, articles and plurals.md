## 2 · Learn (8 min)
Every noun is masculine or feminine, and the little words around it match.

| | masculine | feminine |
|---|---|---|
| the (one) | **el** libro | **la** mesa |
| the (many) | **los** libros | **las** mesas |
| a / some | **un** libro / **unos** libros | **una** mesa / **unas** mesas |

- Usually **-o = masculine, -a = feminine**, but learn the article with each word: **el día, el problema, la mano**.
- Plurals: vowel + **s** (libro → libros) · consonant + **es** (ciudad → ciudades) · **z → ces** (lápiz → lápices).
