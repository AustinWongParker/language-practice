## 2 · Learn (8 min)
Use the subjunctive after phrases of **emotion** or **doubt** about someone else:
- Emotion: **me alegra que, me encanta que, me molesta que, tengo miedo de que, es una pena que**
  - **Me alegra que estés aquí.** = I'm glad you're here.
- Doubt or denial: **dudo que, no creo que, no es verdad que, es posible que**
  - **No creo que llueva.** = I don't think it'll rain.
- Certainty keeps the normal indicative: **Creo que llueve.** · **Es verdad que es caro.** · **No dudo que tienes razón.**
- After **cuando** for the future: **Cuando llegues, llámame.** (Not "cuando llegarás".)

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. I'm glad you're happy.
2. I doubt he knows it.
3. I don't think it's true.
4. When you arrive, call me.
5. I'm afraid we'll lose.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Me alegra que estés feliz.
> 2. Dudo que lo sepa.
> 3. No creo que sea verdad.
> 4. Cuando llegues, llámame.
> 5. Tengo miedo de que perdamos.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿Qué te alegra que pase?** _(What are you glad is happening?)_
- **¿Dudas que puedas hablar español en un año?** _(Do you doubt you can speak Spanish in a year?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.
