## 2 · Learn (8 min)
When one person wants, asks or hopes that **someone else** does something, the second verb goes into the **present subjunctive**. It uses the same forms as the commands you learned in week 18:

| | hablar | comer / vivir | ir | ser | tener |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| yo | hable | coma | vaya | sea | tenga |
| tú | hables | comas | vayas | seas | tengas |
| él / ella | hable | coma | vaya | sea | tenga |
| nosotros | hablemos | comamos | vayamos | seamos | tengamos |
| ellos | hablen | coman | vayan | sean | tengan |

- **Quiero que vengas.** = I want you to come. (Not "quiero tú venir".)
- Same subject = infinitive: **Quiero ir.** Different subject = **que** + subjunctive: **Quiero que vayas.**
- Triggers: **querer que, esperar que, pedir que, necesitar que, ojalá (que)**, and **permitir que** (to let).
- **Ojalá** = I hope / if only: **¡Ojalá no llueva!**

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. I want you to come with me.
2. I hope you have a good trip.
3. She asks me to help her.
4. Hopefully it won't rain!
5. We need you to be here at eight.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Quiero que vengas conmigo.
> 2. Espero que tengas un buen viaje.
> 3. Me pide que la ayude.
> 4. ¡Ojalá no llueva!
> 5. Necesitamos que estés aquí a las ocho.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿Qué quieres que haga yo?** _(What do you want me to do?)_
- **¿Qué esperas que pase este año?** _(What do you hope happens this year?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.
