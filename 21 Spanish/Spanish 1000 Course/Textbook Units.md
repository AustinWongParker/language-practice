# 📝 Textbook Units

Short textbook-style grammar, spelling and pronunciation units, one on each practice day of the [[Spanish 1000 Course]]. Each has a rule with examples and a 6-question exercise with an answer key. Review days quiz you on that week's units, plus one from a few weeks back.

Topics follow a standard beginner-to-intermediate syllabus (cross-checked against [SpanishDict's grammar guide](https://www.spanishdict.com/guide)), timed to come after the grammar they build on.
