## 2 · Learn (8 min)
Start from the **yo** form, drop the -o, and switch to the "other" vowel:
- -ar verbs → **-e / -en**: hablar (hablo) → **hable / hablen**
- -er and -ir verbs → **-a / -an**: comer (como) → **coma / coman**, salir (salgo) → **salga / salgan**, tener (tengo) → **tenga / tengan**
- Irregular: **vaya** (ir), **sea** (ser), **esté** (estar), **dé** (dar), **sepa** (saber).
- Negative tú commands use the same forms with -s: **no hables, no comas, no vayas**.
- Pronouns stick to the end of affirmative commands and go before negative ones: **Siéntese.** · **No se preocupe.**
- You'll hear these all the time on signs and in shops: **Pase, por favor.** · **Espere aquí.**

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. Come in, please (usted).
2. Wait here (ustedes).
3. Don't worry (usted).
4. Don't eat that (tú)!
5. Take a seat (usted).

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)

> 2. Esperen aquí.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿Qué le dirías a un turista en tu ciudad?** _(What would you tell a tourist in your city?)_
- **Dame instrucciones formales para llegar a tu casa.** _(Give me formal directions to your home.)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.
