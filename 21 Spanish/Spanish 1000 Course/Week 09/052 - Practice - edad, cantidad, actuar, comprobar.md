## 3 · Textbook (5 min): The personal "a"
Read the rule out loud, do the exercise, then check the answers. All units: [[Textbook Units]].

When the direct object of a verb is a **specific person** (or a pet you love), put **a** before it. English has nothing like it.

- **Veo a María.** (I see María.) but **Veo la casa.** (I see the house.)
- **Llamo a mi madre.** · **Conozco a tu hermano.** · **Busco a Cooper.**
- With **quién**: **¿A quién llamas?** = Who are you calling?
- **a + el = al**: **Veo al profesor.**

**No personal a:**
- With things and places: **Visito Madrid.** (but **Visito a mi abuela.**)
- With **tener**: **Tengo dos hermanos.**
- With unspecified people: **Busco un médico.** (any doctor) vs **Busco a mi médico.** (my specific one)

**Exercise:** Add the personal a where it's needed.

1. I see my friend. → 
2. I see the car. → 
3. I'm calling the teacher. → 
4. I have three brothers. → 
5. Who do you know here? → 
6. We're visiting Mexico. →
