## 2 · Learn (8 min)
An indirect object is who something is given, said or done **to** or **for**: I give **him** the book.

| pronoun | means |
|---|---|
| **me** | to me |
| **te** | to you |
| **le** | to him / her / you (formal) |
| **nos** | to us |
| **les** | to them / you all |

- **Le doy el libro.** = I give him (or her) the book. Add **a él / a ella** if it's unclear.
- You already use these with **gustar**. Verbs that work the same way: **encantar** (to love), **interesar** (to interest), **importar** (to matter), **doler** (to hurt, o → ue).
- **Me duele la cabeza.** = My head hurts. · **Me duelen los pies.** = My feet hurt.
