## 2 · Learn (8 min)
A direct object receives the action (I see **the book**). Replace it with a pronoun to avoid repeating it:

| pronoun | means |
|---|---|
| **me** | me |
| **te** | you |
| **lo** | him, it (masculine), you (formal, man) |
| **la** | her, it (feminine), you (formal, woman) |
| **nos** | us |
| **los / las** | them (masculine / feminine) |

- The pronoun goes **before** a conjugated verb: **¿El libro? Lo veo.**
- With an infinitive you can also attach it: **Lo quiero comprar = Quiero comprarlo.**

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. I see it (the book).
2. Do you call her every day?
3. We're looking for them (the keys).
4. I want to buy it (the gift).
5. Can you help me?

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Lo veo.
> 2. ¿La llamas todos los días?
> 3. Las buscamos.
> 4. Quiero comprarlo. / Lo quiero comprar.
> 5. ¿Me puedes ayudar? / ¿Puedes ayudarme?

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿Ves a tu familia mucho? ¿La llamas?** _(Do you see your family a lot? Do you call them?)_
- **¿Con quién tienes una buena relación?** _(Who do you have a good relationship with?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.
