## 2 · Learn (8 min)
| | this (near me) | that (near you) | that (over there) |
|---|---|---|---|
| masculine | **este** | **ese** | **aquel** |
| feminine | **esta** | **esa** | **aquella** |
| masculine plural | **estos** | **esos** | **aquellos** |
| feminine plural | **estas** | **esas** | **aquellas** |

- **Esto / eso** (no gender) point to an idea or something you can't name: **¿Qué es esto?** · **Eso es todo.** (That's all.)
- **cerca** = near · **lejos** = far

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. This shirt is new.
2. I want that book (near you).
3. That building over there is very old.
4. What is this?
5. These questions are difficult.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Esta camisa es nueva.
> 2. Quiero ese libro.
> 3. Aquel edificio es muy viejo.
> 4. ¿Qué es esto?
> 5. Estas preguntas son difíciles.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **Señala algo: ¿qué es esto? ¿Y eso?** _(Point at something: what's this? And that?)_
- **¿Qué es importante para ti?** _(What's important to you?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.

Afterwards, add the best correction you got to [[Spanish Mistakes Log]].
