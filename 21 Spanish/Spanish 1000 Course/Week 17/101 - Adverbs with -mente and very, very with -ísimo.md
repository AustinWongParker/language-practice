## 2 · Learn (8 min)
- Take the **feminine** form of the adjective and add **-mente**: rápido → **rápidamente**, fácil → **fácilmente**, perfecto → **perfectamente**.
- An accent stays where it was: **fácilmente**, **rápidamente**.
- Two in a row? Only the last one gets -mente: **clara y lentamente**.
- In speech people often just use the adjective: **habla rápido**.
- **-ísimo** = extremely: **muy bueno → buenísimo**, **muy caro → carísimo**, **muy rico → riquísimo**, **muy poco → poquísimo**.
- Superlative: **el / la más** + adjective: **el más alto de la clase**.

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

3. The food was super tasty.
4. Especially on Mondays.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.
