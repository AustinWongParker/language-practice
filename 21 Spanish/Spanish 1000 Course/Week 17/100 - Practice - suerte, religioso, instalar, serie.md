## 3 · Textbook (5 min): Verb + infinitive phrases
Read the rule out loud, do the exercise, then check the answers. All units: [[Textbook Units]].

Many everyday ideas are a verb + infinitive. Learn them as chunks:

| Phrase | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **ir a** + inf. | going to | **Voy a** comer. |
| **acabar de** + inf. | to have just | **Acabo de** llegar. = I just arrived. |
| **volver a** + inf. | to do again | **Volví a** llamar. = I called again. |
| **dejar de** + inf. | to stop doing | **Dejé de** fumar. |
| **empezar a / ponerse a** + inf. | to start | **Empezó a** llover. |
| **tener que** + inf. | to have to | **Tengo que** irme. |
| **hay que** + inf. | one must, you have to | **Hay que** practicar. |
| **deber** + inf. | should | **Debes** descansar. |
| **soler** + inf. | to usually do | **Suelo** levantarme a las 7. |
| **estar a punto de** + inf. | to be about to | **Estoy a punto de** salir. |
| **seguir** + gerund | to keep doing | **Sigo** estudiando. |

**Exercise:** Say it with one of the phrases.

1. I just ate. → 
2. I usually walk to work. → 
3. He stopped smoking. → 
4. You have to try it. (general) → 
5. I'm about to leave. → 
6. She keeps working. → 

> [!check]- Answers (check after you try)
> 1. Acabo de comer.
> 2. Suelo caminar al trabajo.
> 3. Dejó de fumar.
> 4. Hay que probarlo.
> 5. Estoy a punto de salir.
> 6. Sigue trabajando.
