## 2 · Learn (8 min)
- **que** = that, which, who. The default; it works for people and things: **El libro que leo.** · **La chica que vive aquí.**
- **quien / quienes** = who, after a preposition: **el amigo con quien viajo**.
- **donde** = where: **la ciudad donde nací**.
- **lo que** = what (the thing that): **Lo que quiero es dormir.** · **No entiendo lo que dices.**
- **cuando** = when: **Cuando llegues, llámame.**
- **cuyo / cuya** = whose (formal, mostly in writing): **el hombre cuyo hijo...**
- Unlike English, you can't drop **que**: "the book I read" = **el libro que leí**.

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. The man who works here is my uncle.
2. That's the city where I was born.
3. What I need is a vacation.
4. The friend I travel with.
5. I don't understand what you're saying.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. El hombre que trabaja aquí es mi tío.
> 2. Esa es la ciudad donde nací.
> 3. Lo que necesito son unas vacaciones.
> 4. El amigo con quien viajo.
> 5. No entiendo lo que dices.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **Describe la ciudad donde naciste.** _(Describe the city where you were born.)_
- **¿Qué es lo que más te gusta de tu trabajo?** _(What do you like most about your work?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.
