## 2 · Learn (8 min)
Three common verbs with a **-go** yo form *and* a stem change:

| | tener (have) | venir (come) | decir (say, tell) |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | **tengo** | **vengo** | **digo** |
| tú | tienes | vienes | dices |
| él / ella / usted | tiene | viene | dice |
| nosotros | tenemos | venimos | decimos |
| ellos / ustedes | tienen | vienen | dicen |

**Tener** expressions, where English uses "to be": **tener hambre** (hungry) · **tener sed** (thirsty) · **tener sueño** (sleepy) · **tener frío / calor** (cold / hot) · **tener prisa** (in a hurry) · **tener razón** (right)

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. I'm hungry.
2. Are you coming to the party?
3. She always tells the truth.
4. We're in a hurry.
5. You're right.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Tengo hambre.
> 2. ¿Vienes a la fiesta?
> 3. Ella siempre dice la verdad.
> 4. Tenemos prisa.
> 5. Tienes razón.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿Vienes aquí mucho? ¿Qué dices cuando llegas?** _(Do you come here a lot? What do you say when you arrive?)_
- **¿Tienes hambre? ¿Tienes frío o calor?** _(Are you hungry? Are you cold or hot?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.

Afterwards, add the best correction you got to [[Spanish Mistakes Log]].

> Answer the questions out loud anyway and record yourself on your phone. Listen back once and note one thing to fix. Or book a session with a tutor (italki, Preply) or a language exchange partner (Tandem, HelloTalk).
