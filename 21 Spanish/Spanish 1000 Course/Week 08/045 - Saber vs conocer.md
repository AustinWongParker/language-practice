## 2 · Learn (8 min)
Both mean "to know", but they aren't interchangeable.

| saber | conocer |
|---|---|
| facts and information | people, places, things you're familiar with |
| how to do something (+ infinitive) | meeting someone for the first time |
| **sé**, sabes, sabe, sabemos, saben | **conozco**, conoces, conoce, conocemos, conocen |

- **No sé.** = I don't know. · **Sé nadar.** = I know how to swim.
- **Conozco Madrid.** = I know (have been to) Madrid.
- When the thing you know is a person, add **a**: **Conozco a María.** This "personal a" goes before any person who receives the action.

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. I don't know the answer.
2. Do you know María?
3. I know how to swim.
4. We know the neighborhood well.
5. Do you know where the store is?

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. No sé la respuesta.
> 2. ¿Conoces a María?
> 3. Sé nadar.
> 4. Conocemos bien el barrio.
> 5. ¿Sabes dónde está la tienda?

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿Qué sabes hacer bien? ¿A quién conoces bien?** _(What do you know how to do well? Who do you know well?)_
- **¿Conoces otro país? ¿Cuál?** _(Do you know another country? Which one?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.
