## 2 · Learn (8 min)
| | one thing | many things |
|---|---|---|
| my | **mi** | **mis** |
| your | **tu** | **tus** |
| his / her / your (formal) / their | **su** | **sus** |
| our | **nuestro / nuestra** | **nuestros / nuestras** |
| your (all, Spain) | **vuestro / vuestra** | **vuestros / vuestras** |

The possessive matches the thing owned, not the owner: **nuestra casa, mis hermanos**. **Tu** (your) has no accent; **tú** (you) does.

**Family:** el padre / la madre · los padres (parents) · el hermano / la hermana · el hijo / la hija · el abuelo / la abuela · el tío / la tía · el primo / la prima · la pareja (partner) · la mascota (pet)

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. My parents live in another city.
2. Your sister is very nice.
3. Our house is small.
4. Their children are tall.
5. My dog's name is Cooper.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Mis padres viven en otra ciudad.
> 2. Tu hermana es muy simpática.
> 3. Nuestra casa es pequeña.
> 4. Sus hijos son altos.
> 5. Mi perro se llama Cooper.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **Habla de tu madre o tu hermano.** _(Talk about your mother or brother.)_
- **¿Cómo es tu familia? ¿Es grande?** _(What's your family like? Is it big?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.
