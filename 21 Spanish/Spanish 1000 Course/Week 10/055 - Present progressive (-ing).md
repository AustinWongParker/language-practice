## 2 · Learn (8 min)
For something happening **right now**, use **estar + the -ing form** (the gerund):

- **-ar → -ando**: hablar → **hablando**
- **-er / -ir → -iendo**: comer → **comiendo**, vivir → **viviendo**
- Spelling and stem changes: leer → **leyendo**, dormir → **durmiendo**, pedir → **pidiendo**, decir → **diciendo**

**Estoy estudiando.** = I'm studying (right now). Spanish doesn't use this for the future or for habits: "I'm working tomorrow" is **Trabajo mañana**.

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

2. What are you doing?
3. The dog is sleeping.
4. We're waiting for the bus.
5. She's reading a book.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Estoy estudiando ahora mismo.
> 2. ¿Qué estás haciendo?

> 4. Estamos esperando el autobús.
> 5. Está leyendo un libro.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿Qué estás haciendo ahora mismo?** _(What are you doing right now?)_
- **¿Qué es difícil para ti?** _(What's hard for you?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.
