## 2 · Learn (8 min)
**¿Qué tiempo hace?** = What's the weather like?

- **hace** + noun: **hace calor** (hot) · **hace frío** (cold) · **hace sol** (sunny) · **hace viento** (windy) · **hace buen / mal tiempo** (nice / bad weather)
- **llueve** = it rains, it's raining · **nieva** = it snows · **está nublado** = it's cloudy
- Add **mucho**: **hace mucho calor** = it's very hot

**Seasons:** la primavera (spring) · el verano (summer) · el otoño (fall) · el invierno (winter)

Don't mix these up with **tener calor / frío** from lesson 43: **hace frío** is the weather, **tengo frío** is how you feel.

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. What's the weather like?
2. It's very hot today.
3. It's raining.
4. In winter it's cold and it snows.
5. I like summer because it's sunny.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. ¿Qué tiempo hace?
> 2. Hoy hace mucho calor.
> 3. Llueve. / Está lloviendo.
> 4. En invierno hace frío y nieva.
> 5. Me gusta el verano porque hace sol.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿Qué tiempo hace hoy?** _(What's the weather like today?)_
- **¿Cuál es tu estación favorita? ¿Por qué?** _(What's your favorite season? Why?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.
