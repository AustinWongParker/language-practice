## 2 · Learn (8 min)
| | estar | poder | decir |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | **estuve** | **pude** | **dije** |
| tú | **estuviste** | **pudiste** | **dijiste** |
| él / ella / usted | **estuvo** | **pudo** | **dijo** |
| nosotros | **estuvimos** | **pudimos** | **dijimos** |
| ellos / ustedes | **estuvieron** | **pudieron** | **dijeron** |

| | ver | dar |
|---|---|---|
| yo | **vi** | **di** |
| tú | **viste** | **diste** |
| él / ella / usted | **vio** | **dio** |
| nosotros | **vimos** | **dimos** |
| ellos / ustedes | **vieron** | **dieron** |

The first group follows one pattern: an irregular stem plus **-e, -iste, -o, -imos, -ieron** (but **dijeron**, not *dijieron*).

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. I was at home all day.
2. I couldn't sleep.
3. What did she say?
4. We saw a movie.
5. I gave him the keys.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Estuve en casa todo el día.
> 2. No pude dormir.
> 3. ¿Qué dijo?
> 4. Vimos una película.
> 5. Le di las llaves.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿Qué te dijo tu jefe o tu amigo ayer?** _(What did your boss or friend tell you yesterday?)_
- **¿Qué viste en la televisión ayer?** _(What did you watch on TV yesterday?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.

Afterwards, add the best correction you got to [[Spanish Mistakes Log]].

> [!info]- No partner today?
> Answer the questions out loud anyway and record yourself on your phone. Listen back once and note one thing to fix. Or book a session with a tutor (italki, Preply) or a language exchange partner (Tandem, HelloTalk).
