## 2 · Learn (8 min)
| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **ayer** | yesterday |
| **anteayer** | the day before yesterday |
| **anoche** | last night |
| **el lunes pasado** | last Monday |
| **la semana pasada** | last week |
| **el mes pasado** | last month |
| **el año pasado** | last year |
| **hace dos días** | two days ago |

**Hace + amount of time** = ago: **hace una semana** (a week ago), **hace mucho tiempo** (a long time ago).

**Telling a story:** **primero** → **luego** → **después** → **de repente** (suddenly) → **al final** (in the end). **Por fin** = finally, at last.

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. Two days ago I went to the beach.
2. Last year we lived in another city.
3. The day before yesterday I cooked chicken.
4. Finally, I finished the book.
5. Last Monday she didn't work.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Hace dos días fui a la playa.
> 2. El año pasado vivimos en otra ciudad.
> 3. Anteayer cociné pollo.
> 4. Por fin terminé el libro.
> 5. El lunes pasado no trabajó.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿Qué hiciste anoche? ¿Y anteayer?** _(What did you do last night? And the day before yesterday?)_
- **¿Qué hiciste hace un año?** _(What were you doing a year ago?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.

Afterwards, add the best correction you got to [[Spanish Mistakes Log]].

> Answer the questions out loud anyway and record yourself on your phone. Listen back once and note one thing to fix. Or book a session with a tutor (italki, Preply) or a language exchange partner (Tandem, HelloTalk).
