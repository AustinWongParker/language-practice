# 🔁 Anki Setup

**Anki** is a free flashcard app that uses spaced repetition: it shows each word just before you'd forget it, so reviews stay short and words stick long-term. Part of the [[Spanish 1000 Course]].

## 1 · Install
- **Windows / Mac:** free from [apps.ankiweb.net](https://apps.ankiweb.net)
- **Android:** AnkiDroid (free) · **iPhone:** AnkiMobile (paid), or use the free web version at [ankiweb.net](https://ankiweb.net)
- Make a free AnkiWeb account and press **Sync** to keep your phone and computer in step.

## 2 · Import the deck
1. In Anki on your computer: **File → Import**.
2. Choose `Spanish 1000 - Anki import.txt`. It's in `C:\Obsidian\AWP_Vault_Sync\20 Languages 🌎\21 Spanish\Spanish 1000 Course`. (Obsidian hides .txt files, so it won't show in the sidebar.)
3. Check the preview shows **Spanish 1000** as the deck and **Basic** as the note type, then click **Import**. You should get 1000 cards.

Each card shows the Spanish word and a short phrase. Flip it to see the English. Cards are in course order and tagged by week and lesson (`Spanish1000::lesson012`).

## 3 · Settings (do this once)
Click the ⚙️ next to **Spanish 1000 → Options**:
- **New cards/day: 10**, one lesson's worth.
- **Maximum reviews/day: 200**
- **Display Order → New/review order: Show after reviews**. This lets you stop before the new cards on review days.
- Leave everything else as it is.

## 4 · Hear every card (recommended)
Make Anki read each card in Spanish:
1. Click **Browse**, select any Spanish 1000 card, then click **Cards…**
2. In **Front Template**, add this on a new line at the end: `{{tts es_ES:Front}}`
3. Save. Anki now reads the word and phrase aloud using your device's Spanish voice. (On Windows, add a Spanish voice under **Settings → Time & language → Speech** if you don't hear one.)

## 5 · Daily routine
- **Learning days:** do every due card, then the 10 new ones. Answer honestly: **Again** if you didn't know it, **Good** if you did. Use **Hard** and **Easy** rarely.
- **Review days:** do the due cards only. Stop when only new (blue) cards are left.
- **Free day:** a quick review session still helps a lot.
- Say the Spanish out loud before you flip the card.
- Too many reviews piling up? Lower new cards to 7 for a week. Don't skip days: missed days pile up.

> [!tip] Anki and the lessons stay in step
> Anki adds 10 new cards each day you study, and each learning day in the course teaches the same 10 words. If you miss a lesson, skip Anki's new cards that day too (do reviews only), and the two will match.
