## 2 · Learn (8 min)
Adjectives usually go **after** the noun and match it in gender and number:

| | one | many |
|---|---|---|
| masculine | un perro **pequeño** | unos perros **pequeños** |
| feminine | una casa **pequeña** | unas casas **pequeñas** |

- Adjectives ending in **-e** or a consonant don't change for gender: **grande, fácil** → plural **grandes, fáciles**.
- **muy** = very: **muy bonito**.
- **bueno / malo** can go before a masculine noun and shorten: **un buen día, un mal día**.

- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.
