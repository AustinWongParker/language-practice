## 2 · Learn (8 min)
**Meals:** **el desayuno** (breakfast) · **el almuerzo** (lunch) · **la cena** (dinner). Verbs: **desayunar, almorzar, cenar**.

**At a restaurant**
- **Quisiera…** = I'd like… (polite) · **Para mí…** = For me…
- **¿Me trae…, por favor?** = Could you bring me…?
- **¿Qué me recomienda?** = What do you recommend?
- **La cuenta, por favor.** = The check, please.

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. I'd like chicken with rice, please.
2. For me, a salad.
3. The check, please.
4. We have dinner at eight.
5. Do you eat meat or fish?

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Quisiera pollo con arroz, por favor.
> 2. Para mí, una ensalada.
> 3. La cuenta, por favor.
> 4. Cenamos a las ocho.
> 5. ¿Comes carne o pescado?

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿Cuál es tu comida favorita?** _(What's your favorite food?)_
- **Pide algo en un restaurante: yo soy el camarero.** _(Order something at a restaurant: I'm the waiter.)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.

Afterwards, add the best correction you got to [[Spanish Mistakes Log]].
