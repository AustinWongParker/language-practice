## 2 · Learn (8 min)
**Gustar** works backwards from English: the thing you like is the subject. **Me gusta el café** = coffee is pleasing to me.

- One thing or an action → **gusta**: **Me gusta el café. Me gusta correr.**
- Several things → **gustan**: **Me gustan los perros.**
- Who likes it: **me** (me) · **te** (you) · **le** (him / her / you formal) · **nos** (us) · **les** (them / you all)
- Negative: **No me gusta.** · Agreeing: **A mí también** (me too) · **A mí tampoco** (me neither)

## 3 · Listen (7 min)
Watch a **Superbeginner or Beginner** video on [Dreaming Spanish](https://www.dreaming.com/spanish) (or its [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@DreamingSpanish) channel). Don't translate in your head; follow along from the pictures and gestures. The site logs your hours for you.
**Listen for:** me gusta / no me gusta: what do people like?

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. I like coffee.
2. Do you like dogs?
3. We like to run.
4. She doesn't like the music.
5. I like books a lot.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Me gusta el café.
> 2. ¿Te gustan los perros?
> 3. Nos gusta correr.
> 4. No le gusta la música.
> 5. Me gustan mucho los libros.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿Qué te gusta hacer por la tarde?** _(What do you like to do in the afternoon?)_
- **¿Te gusta el cine? ¿Qué tipo de música te gusta?** _(Do you like movies? What kind of music do you like?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.

Afterwards, add the best correction you got to [[Spanish Mistakes Log]].
