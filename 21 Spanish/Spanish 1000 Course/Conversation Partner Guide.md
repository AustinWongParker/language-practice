# 🗣️ Conversation Partner Guide

Thanks for helping! This is for whoever does the daily 10-minute conversation in the [[Spanish 1000 Course]]. You don't need to speak Spanish well: every lesson has an answer key, and the questions come with English translations.

## The 10 minutes
**1 · Warm-up drill (3 min)**
- Open today's lesson to **Talk with your partner**.
- Read each English line out loud. Your learner answers in Spanish.
- Check against the **Answer key** (click to open it). Close enough counts. If they miss one, give the answer, and come back to it at the end.

**2 · Conversation (7 min)**
- Ask the questions in the lesson (in Spanish if you can, in English if not). Your learner answers in Spanish, in full sentences.
- Follow up with simple questions: **¿Por qué?** (why?), **¿Y después?** (and then?), **¿Con quién?** (with whom?), **¿Cómo es?** (what's it like?)
- Then swap: they ask you the same questions.

**Review days** (every 6th lesson) have one longer speaking task instead. Just follow the instructions in the lesson.

## Tips
- **Let them struggle a little.** Wait a few seconds before helping. Searching for a word is how it gets learned.
- **Don't correct every mistake mid-sentence.** Note one or two, and mention them at the end. They'll add the best one to their mistakes log.
- **Speak slowly and simply**, and use gestures. If they're stuck, rephrase rather than translate.
- **Keep it fun.** Laughing at mistakes is fine; conversations people enjoy are the ones they remember.
- A few minutes over or under is fine.

## Useful phrases
| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Más despacio, por favor. | Slower, please. |
| ¿Cómo se dice ...? | How do you say ...? |
| ¿Puedes repetir? | Can you repeat? |
| Otra vez. | Again. |
| ¡Muy bien! / ¡Perfecto! | Very good! / Perfect! |
| Casi. | Almost. |
| Se dice ... | You say ... |

## No partner available?
- **Language exchange (free):** Tandem or HelloTalk. Swap 10 minutes of Spanish for 10 minutes of English.
- **Tutor (paid):** italki or Preply. Many offer short, cheap sessions.
- **Solo:** answer the questions out loud and record yourself. Listen back once, and note one thing to fix.
