## 3 · Textbook (5 min): If-clauses and guessing with the future
Read the rule out loud, do the exercise, then check the answers. All units: [[Textbook Units]].

**If-clauses (real possibilities):** **si** + present, then present, future or a command.
- **Si llueve, me quedo en casa.** · **Si tengo tiempo, te llamaré.** · **Si puedes, ven.**
- Never the future after **si**: ~~Si lloverá~~ → **Si llueve**.

**Imagined situations** use the conditional in the main clause (with the past subjunctive after si, which you'll meet later): **Si tuviera dinero, viajaría.** = If I had money, I'd travel. Learn this one as a set phrase for now.

**Guessing with the future and conditional:** Spanish uses the future to wonder about the present, and the conditional for the past:
- **¿Dónde estará Ana?** = I wonder where Ana is. · **Estará en casa.** = She's probably at home.
- **¿Qué hora será?** = I wonder what time it is.
- **Serían las diez.** = It was probably about ten.

**Exercise:** Say it in Spanish.

1. If it rains, I'll stay home. → 
2. If you can, call me. → 
3. If you study, you'll pass. → 
4. I wonder where my keys are. → 
5. It's probably about three o'clock. → 
6. If I had money, I'd travel. →

> 2. Si puedes, llámame.
> 3. Si estudias, aprobarás.
> 4. ¿Dónde estarán mis llaves?
> 5. Serán las tres.
> 6. Si tuviera dinero, viajaría.
