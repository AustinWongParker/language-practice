## 2 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

Speak for **two minutes** without notes: who you are, your routine, your family, what you did last weekend, what you were like as a kid, your plans and your hopes for the future. Your partner then asks you questions for the rest of the time.

**Record it**, then listen to your week 1 recording. Write down three things you can do now that you couldn't then.

Afterwards, add the corrections you got to [[Spanish Mistakes Log]].

> [!info]- No partner today?
> Do the task alone out loud and record it on your phone. Or book a tutor (italki, Preply) or a language exchange (Tandem, HelloTalk).
