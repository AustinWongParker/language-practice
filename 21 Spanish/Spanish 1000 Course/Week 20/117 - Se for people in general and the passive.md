## 2 · Learn (8 min)
- **Impersonal se** = "you", "people", "one": **¿Cómo se dice...?** = How do you say...? · **Aquí se come bien.** = You eat well here.
- **Passive se**: the verb agrees with the thing: **Se vende casa.** · **Se venden casas.** · **Se habla español.**
- **Accidental se** = "it happened to me": **Se me olvidó.** = I forgot (it slipped my mind). · **Se me cayó el vaso.** = I dropped the glass. · **Se me rompió.** = It broke on me.
- You'll see these on signs: **Se busca empleado** (help wanted), **Se alquila** (for rent), **Se prohíbe fumar** (no smoking).
