## 2 · Learn (8 min)
**haber** (present) + past participle = "have done".

| | haber | + participle |
|---|---|---|
| yo | **he** | hablado |
| tú | **has** | comido |
| él / ella / usted | **ha** | vivido |
| nosotros | **hemos** | |
| ellos / ustedes | **han** | |

- Participles: **-ar → -ado** (hablado), **-er / -ir → -ido** (comido, vivido).
- Common irregulars: **hecho** (hacer), **dicho** (decir), **visto** (ver), **puesto** (poner), **escrito** (escribir), **abierto** (abrir), **vuelto** (volver), **muerto** (morir).
- Use it with **hoy, esta semana, este año, ya, todavía no, nunca**: **Hoy he trabajado mucho.**
- In much of Latin America people use the preterite instead (**hoy trabajé**). Both are understood everywhere.
- Participles also work as adjectives: **la puerta está abierta**, **la tienda está cerrada**.

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. I have eaten already.
2. Have you ever been to Mexico?
3. We haven't seen that movie yet.
4. What have you done today?
5. The store is closed.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Ya he comido.
> 2. ¿Has estado en México alguna vez?
> 3. Todavía no hemos visto esa película.
> 4. ¿Qué has hecho hoy?
> 5. La tienda está cerrada.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿Qué has hecho hoy?** _(What have you done today?)_
- **¿Has estado en otro país?** _(Have you ever been to another country?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.
