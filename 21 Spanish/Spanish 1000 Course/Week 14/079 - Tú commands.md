## 2 · Learn (8 min)
To tell a friend to do something, use the **él / ella** present form:

- hablar → **habla** · comer → **come** · escribir → **escribe** · esperar → **espera**

Eight common irregulars: **di** (decir) · **haz** (hacer) · **ve** (ir) · **pon** (poner) · **sal** (salir) · **sé** (ser) · **ten** (tener) · **ven** (venir)

Attach pronouns to the end, adding an accent to keep the stress: **llama → llámame** (call me) · **ayuda → ayúdame** (help me) · **di → dime** (tell me)

"Don't …" uses a different form (**no hables**). That's the subjunctive, a good next step after this course.

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. Speak more slowly, please.
2. Come here!
3. Do your homework.
4. Call me tomorrow.
5. Wait a moment.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Habla más despacio, por favor.
> 2. ¡Ven aquí!
> 3. Haz la tarea.
> 4. Llámame mañana.
> 5. Espera un momento.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **Dame instrucciones para hacer un café.** _(Give me instructions to make a coffee.)_
- **Dame un consejo para descansar mejor.** _(Give me some advice for resting better.)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.
