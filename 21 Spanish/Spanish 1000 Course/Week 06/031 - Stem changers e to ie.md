## 2 · Learn (8 min)
Some verbs change their stem vowel when it's stressed: **e → ie**. The change happens in every form **except nosotros and vosotros**. Draw a line around the forms that change and you'll see why they're called "boot verbs".

| | pensar (to think) |
|---|---|
| yo | **pienso** |
| tú | **piensas** |
| él / ella / usted | **piensa** |
| nosotros | **pensamos** |
| vosotros | **pensáis** |
| ellos / ustedes | **piensan** |

Same pattern: **querer** (quiero), **empezar** (empiezo), **preferir** (prefiero), **entender** (entiendo), **cerrar** (cierro).

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. I think it's easy.
2. The class starts early.
3. Do you prefer coffee or tea?
4. We understand the movie.
5. They close the store at ten.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Pienso que es fácil.
> 2. La clase empieza temprano.
> 3. ¿Prefieres café o té?
> 4. Entendemos la película.
> 5. Cierran la tienda a las diez.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿Qué prefieres: el campo o la ciudad?** _(What do you prefer: the countryside or the city?)_
- **¿Entiendes más español que antes?** _(Do you understand more Spanish than before?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.

Afterwards, add the best correction you got to [[Spanish Mistakes Log]].
