## 2 · Learn (8 min)
**e → i** happens only in -ir verbs, in the same boot shape:

| | pedir (to ask for, order) |
|---|---|
| yo | **pido** |
| tú | **pides** |
| él / ella / usted | **pide** |
| nosotros | **pedimos** |
| vosotros | **pedís** |
| ellos / ustedes | **piden** |

Same pattern: **servir** (sirvo), **repetir** (repito), **seguir** (sigo; note the spelling).

**Jugar** is the only **u → ue** verb: **juego, juegas, juega, jugamos, jugáis, juegan**. Sports take **a**: **Juego al pickleball.**

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. I always order coffee.
2. Can you repeat the question?
3. We play pickleball on Saturdays.
4. The restaurant serves fish.
5. She follows the plan.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Siempre pido café.
> 2. ¿Puedes repetir la pregunta?
> 3. Jugamos al pickleball los sábados.
> 4. El restaurante sirve pescado.
> 5. Ella sigue el plan.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿A qué juegas? ¿Juegas algún deporte?** _(What do you play? Do you play any sports?)_
- **¿Qué pides cuando no entiendes? (¿Puede repetir?)** _(What do you ask when you don't understand?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.
