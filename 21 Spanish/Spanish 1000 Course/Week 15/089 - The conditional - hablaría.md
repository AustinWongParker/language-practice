## 2 · Learn (8 min)
Same stems as the future, with these endings:

| | hablar |
|---|---|
| yo | hablar**ía** |
| tú | hablar**ías** |
| él / ella / usted | hablar**ía** |
| nosotros | hablar**íamos** |
| ellos / ustedes | hablar**ían** |

- Same irregular stems: **tendría, podría, haría, diría, saldría, vendría, querría, sabría**.
- Polite requests: **¿Podrías ayudarme?** · **Me gustaría un café.** · **Querría...**
- Advice: **Yo que tú, no lo haría.** = If I were you, I wouldn't do it.
- "Would" for past habits is the imperfect, not the conditional: "When I was a kid I would play..." = **jugaba**.

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. I would like a coffee, please.
2. Could you help me?
3. What would you do with a million dollars?
4. We would live near the sea.
5. If I were you, I'd go to the doctor.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Me gustaría un café, por favor.
> 2. ¿Podrías ayudarme?
> 3. ¿Qué harías con un millón de dólares?
> 4. Viviríamos cerca del mar.
> 5. Yo que tú, iría al médico.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿Qué harías con un millón de dólares?** _(What would you do with a million dollars?)_
- **¿Podrías vivir lejos de tu familia?** _(Could you live far from your family?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.

Afterwards, add the best correction you got to [[Spanish Mistakes Log]].
