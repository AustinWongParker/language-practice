## 3 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

Talk about the future: what you'll do next year (**haré, viajaré**) and what you would do if you had more time or money (**haría, viviría**). Your partner asks **¿Y por qué?** after each answer.

Afterwards, add the corrections you got to [[Spanish Mistakes Log]].
