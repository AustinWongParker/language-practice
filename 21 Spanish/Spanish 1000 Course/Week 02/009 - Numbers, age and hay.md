## 2 · Learn (8 min)
**0–15:** cero, uno, dos, tres, cuatro, cinco, seis, siete, ocho, nueve, diez, once, doce, trece, catorce, quince
**16–19:** dieciséis, diecisiete, dieciocho, diecinueve
**20–29:** veinte, veintiuno, veintidós, veintitrés, veinticuatro, veinticinco, veintiséis, veintisiete, veintiocho, veintinueve
**30:** treinta (31 = treinta y uno, 32 = treinta y dos…)

**Age uses tener** (to have), not ser: **Tengo 30 años** = I'm 30 (literally "I have 30 years"). **¿Cuántos años tienes?** = How old are you?

**Hay** = there is / there are, the same word for one or many: **Hay un perro. Hay tres perros.** · **¿Cuántos libros hay?**

## 3 · Listen (7 min)
Watch a **Superbeginner** video on [Dreaming Spanish](https://www.dreaming.com/spanish) (or its [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@DreamingSpanish) channel). Don't translate in your head; follow along from the pictures and gestures. The site logs your hours for you.
**Listen for:** numbers; try to catch every one you hear.

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. How old are you?
2. I'm 28 years old.
3. There are two dogs in the house.
4. How many books are there?
5. There are fifteen people here.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. ¿Cuántos años tienes?
> 2. Tengo veintiocho años.
> 3. Hay dos perros en la casa.
> 4. ¿Cuántos libros hay?
> 5. Hay quince personas aquí.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿Cuántos años tienes? ¿Y tu hermano o tu amigo?** _(How old are you? And your sibling or friend?)_
- **¿Cuántas personas hay en tu casa?** _(How many people are in your home?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.

Afterwards, add the best correction you got to [[Spanish Mistakes Log]].
