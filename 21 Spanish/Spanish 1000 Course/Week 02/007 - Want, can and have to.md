## 2 · Learn (8 min)
Three verbs that take another verb straight after them, in its "to ___" form (the infinitive). You covered these in [[Expressing Desire, Ability and Obligation in Spanish]].

| | querer (want) | poder (can) | tener que (have to) |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | **quiero** | **puedo** | **tengo que** |
| tú | **quieres** | **puedes** | **tienes que** |
| él / ella / usted | **quiere** | **puede** | **tiene que** |
| nosotros | **queremos** | **podemos** | **tenemos que** |
| ellos / ustedes | **quieren** | **pueden** | **tienen que** |

- **Quiero aprender español.** · **¿Puedes venir?** · **Tengo que trabajar.**
- Notice the vowel change (**e → ie**, **o → ue**) everywhere except **nosotros**. You'll meet more verbs like this in week 6.
- Negative: **No puedo salir.**

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. I want to learn Spanish.
2. Can you practice today?
3. I have to work tomorrow.
4. We want to eat now.
5. She can't go out tonight.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Quiero aprender español.
> 2. ¿Puedes practicar hoy?
> 3. Tengo que trabajar mañana.
> 4. Queremos comer ahora.
> 5. Ella no puede salir esta noche.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿Qué quieres hacer este fin de semana?** _(What do you want to do this weekend?)_
- **¿Qué tienes que hacer mañana?** _(What do you have to do tomorrow?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.

Afterwards, add the best correction you got to [[Spanish Mistakes Log]].

> [!info]- No partner today?
> Answer the questions out loud anyway and record yourself on your phone. Listen back once and note one thing to fix. Or book a session with a tutor (italki, Preply) or a language exchange partner (Tandem, HelloTalk).
