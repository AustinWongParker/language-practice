## 2 · Learn (8 min)
Most verbs end in **-ar**. Drop the -ar and add the ending for who's doing it:

| | hablar (to speak) | ending |
|---|---|---|
| yo | **hablo** | -o |
| tú | **hablas** | -as |
| él / ella / usted | **habla** | -a |
| nosotros | **hablamos** | -amos |
| vosotros | **habláis** | -áis |
| ellos / ustedes | **hablan** | -an |

- One form covers three English ones: **hablo** = I speak / I'm speaking / I do speak.
- **a + el** joins into **al**: **Paseo al perro** = I walk the dog.

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. I work every day.
2. Do you speak Spanish?
3. I walk the dog every morning.
4. She cooks dinner.
5. They need to study more.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Trabajo todos los días.
> 2. ¿Hablas español?
> 3. Paseo al perro todas las mañanas.
> 4. Ella cocina la cena.
> 5. Necesitan estudiar más.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿Trabajas o estudias? ¿Dónde?** _(Do you work or study? Where?)_
- **¿Qué música escuchas?** _(What music do you listen to?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.

Afterwards, add the best correction you got to [[Spanish Mistakes Log]].

> [!info]- No partner today?
> Answer the questions out loud anyway and record yourself on your phone. Listen back once and note one thing to fix. Or book a session with a tutor (italki, Preply) or a language exchange partner (Tandem, HelloTalk).
