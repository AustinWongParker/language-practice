## 3 · Textbook (5 min): Prepositions and prepositional pronouns
Read the rule out loud, do the exercise, then check the answers. All units: [[Textbook Units]].

| | Main uses | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **a** | to (movement), at (time), personal a | Voy **a** casa. **A** las tres. |
| **en** | in, on, at (location), by (transport) | **en** la mesa, **en** casa, **en** coche |
| **de** | of, from, made of, possession | Soy **de** Texas. El libro **de** Ana. |
| **con** | with | café **con** leche |
| **desde... hasta** | from... until | **desde** las nueve **hasta** las cinco |
| **entre** | between, among | **entre** tú y yo |
| **sin** | without | **sin** azúcar |

**After a preposition, use these pronouns:** **mí, ti**, él, ella, usted, nosotros, ellos: **para mí**, **sin ti**, **de él**.
- With **con**: **conmigo** (with me), **contigo** (with you).
- After **entre** and **según**, use **yo / tú**: **entre tú y yo**.

**Verbs with a fixed preposition** (learn them as a set): **pensar en** (think about), **soñar con** (dream of), **depender de**, **casarse con**, **enamorarse de**, **llegar a** (arrive in), **tratar de** (try to).

**Exercise:** Fill in the preposition or pronoun.
