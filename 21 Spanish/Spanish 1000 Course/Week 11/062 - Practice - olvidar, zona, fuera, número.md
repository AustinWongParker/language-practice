## 3 · Textbook (5 min): Big numbers, years and ordinals
Read the rule out loud, do the exercise, then check the answers. All units: [[Textbook Units]].

| | |
|---|---|
| 100 | **cien** (exactly 100), **ciento** + more: ciento uno, ciento veinte |
| 200–900 | dosc**ientos**, tresc**ientos**, cuatroc**ientos**, **quinientos**, seisc**ientos**, **setecientos**, ochoc**ientos**, **novecientos** |
| 1,000 | **mil** (never "un mil"); 2,000 = **dos mil** |
| 1,000,000 | **un millón**; 2,000,000 = **dos millones** |

- Hundreds agree with feminine nouns: **doscientas personas**, **quinientas páginas**.
- **Millón** takes **de** before a noun: **un millón de dólares**.
- **Y** only goes between tens and units: **ciento cuarenta y dos** (142), **dos mil veinticinco** (2025).
- Years are read as full numbers: **1997 = mil novecientos noventa y siete**.
- **Ordinals** (first to tenth): primero, segundo, tercero, cuarto, quinto, sexto, séptimo, octavo, noveno, décimo. They agree: **la segunda vez**. After tenth, people usually just use the number: **el piso doce**.
- Dates use plain numbers, except optionally the 1st: **el 4 de julio**, **el primero (or uno) de mayo**.
