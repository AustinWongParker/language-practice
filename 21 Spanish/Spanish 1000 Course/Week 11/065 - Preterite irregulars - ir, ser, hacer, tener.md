## 2 · Learn (8 min)
The most common verbs are irregular in the preterite, and these forms have **no accents**.

| | ir / ser | hacer | tener |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | **fui** | **hice** | **tuve** |
| tú | **fuiste** | **hiciste** | **tuviste** |
| él / ella / usted | **fue** | **hizo** | **tuvo** |
| nosotros | **fuimos** | **hicimos** | **tuvimos** |
| ellos / ustedes | **fueron** | **hicieron** | **tuvieron** |

- **Ir** and **ser** are identical in the preterite: **Fui al gimnasio** (I went) vs **Fue divertido** (it was fun). Context decides.
- **¿Qué hiciste?** = What did you do?

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. I went to the gym yesterday.
2. What did you do last weekend?
3. The party was fun.
4. We had an exam.
5. She made dinner.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Ayer fui al gimnasio.
> 2. ¿Qué hiciste el fin de semana pasado?
> 3. La fiesta fue divertida.
> 4. Tuvimos un examen.
> 5. Ella hizo la cena.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿Adónde fuiste el fin de semana?** _(Where did you go on the weekend?)_
- **¿Qué hiciste el diciembre pasado?** _(What did you do last December?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.
