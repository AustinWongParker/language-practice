## 2 · Learn (8 min)
**-er** and **-ir** verbs share one set of preterite endings:

| | comer | vivir | ending |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | **comí** | **viví** | -í |
| tú | **comiste** | **viviste** | -iste |
| él / ella / usted | **comió** | **vivió** | -ió |
| nosotros | **comimos** | **vivimos** | -imos |
| vosotros | **comisteis** | **vivisteis** | -isteis |
| ellos / ustedes | **comieron** | **vivieron** | -ieron |

- **leer** and **creer** use a **y**: **leyó, leyeron** · **creyó, creyeron**.
- -ir stem changers change in the él and ellos forms: **pidió, pidieron** · **durmió, durmieron**.

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. I ate at a restaurant.
2. Did you write the message?
3. We went out last night.
4. They learned a lot.
5. He read the book.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Comí en un restaurante.
> 2. ¿Escribiste el mensaje?
> 3. Salimos anoche.
> 4. Aprendieron mucho.
> 5. Leyó el libro.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿Qué comiste ayer?** _(What did you eat yesterday?)_
- **¿Qué descubriste esta semana?** _(What did you discover this week?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.

Afterwards, add the best correction you got to [[Spanish Mistakes Log]].
