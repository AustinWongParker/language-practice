## 1 · Review (7 min)
- **Anki:** do every due card, then today's 10 new cards. See [[Anki Setup]].
- **Quick recall:** say what each word means, and use one in a sentence: **un** · **lo**

> [!check]- Recall answers
> - **un** = a, an (masc.) ([[002 - Practice - y, a, no, se|lesson 2]])
> - **lo** = it, him (object); the (+ adjective) ([[002 - Practice - y, a, no, se|lesson 2]])

## 3 · Listen (8 min)
Watch a **Superbeginner** video on [Dreaming Spanish](https://www.dreaming.com/spanish) (or its [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@DreamingSpanish) channel). Don't translate in your head; follow along from the pictures and gestures. The site logs your hours for you.
**Listen for:** any of today's words, especially **por**, **con**, **todo**, **más**.

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads each English phrase; you say it in Spanish. Get one wrong? Try it again at the end.

1. in the morning
2. with milk
3. all day
4. more or less
5. for you
6. as always
7. if you want
8. can I come in?
9. give me a minute
10. I tell him/her

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. por la mañana
> 2. con leche
> 3. todo el día
> 4. más o menos
> 5. para ti
> 6. como siempre
> 7. si quieres
> 8. ¿puedo pasar?
> 9. dame un minuto
> 10. le digo

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿Qué puedes hacer en español?** _(What can you do in Spanish?)_
- **¿Para qué estudias español?** _(Why are you studying Spanish?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.

Afterwards, add the best correction you got to [[Spanish Mistakes Log]].

> [!info]- No partner today?
> Answer the questions out loud anyway and record yourself on your phone. Listen back once and note one thing to fix. Or book a session with a tutor (italki, Preply) or a language exchange partner (Tandem, HelloTalk).
