## 2 · Learn (8 min)
**Saying hi**
- **Hola** works any time of day.
- **Buenos días** (morning) · **Buenas tardes** (afternoon) · **Buenas noches** (evening and night)

**How are you?**
- **¿Cómo estás?** to a friend · **¿Cómo está usted?** to someone you'd be formal with
- Answers: **Estoy bien / muy bien / más o menos / cansado** (**cansada** for a woman), then **¿Y tú?**

**Names**
- **Me llamo …** = My name is … · **¿Cómo te llamas?** = What's your name?
- **Mucho gusto** = Nice to meet you

**Goodbye:** **Adiós** · **Hasta luego** (see you later) · **Hasta mañana** (see you tomorrow) · **Nos vemos** (see you)

> [!note] Pronunciation check
> Vowels are always short and pure: a (ah), e (eh), i (ee), o (oh), u (oo). **h** is silent (**hola** sounds like "ola"), **ll** sounds like y, and **ñ** like "ny".

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. Good morning! How are you?
2. I'm very well, thanks. And you?
3. My name is Austin. What's your name?
4. Nice to meet you.
5. I'm tired today. See you tomorrow!

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. ¡Buenos días! ¿Cómo estás?
> 2. Estoy muy bien, gracias. ¿Y tú?
> 3. Me llamo Austin. ¿Cómo te llamas?
> 4. Mucho gusto.
> 5. Hoy estoy cansado (cansada). ¡Hasta mañana!

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¡Hola! ¿Cómo estás hoy?** _(Hi! How are you today?)_
- **¿Cómo te llamas? ¿Y cómo está tu familia?** _(What's your name? And how's your family?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.

Afterwards, add the best correction you got to [[Spanish Mistakes Log]].

> [!info]- No partner today?
> Answer the questions out loud anyway and record yourself on your phone. Listen back once and note one thing to fix. Or book a session with a tutor (italki, Preply) or a language exchange partner (Tandem, HelloTalk).
