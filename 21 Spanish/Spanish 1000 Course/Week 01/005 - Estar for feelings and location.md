## 2 · Learn (8 min)
**Estar** (also "to be") is for how something *is* right now (feelings, states) and *where* it is.

| | estar |
|---|---|
| yo | **estoy** |
| tú | **estás** |
| él / ella / usted | **está** |
| nosotros | **estamos** |
| vosotros | **estáis** |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | **están** |

- Feelings: **Estoy contento** (happy) · **¿Estás ocupada?** (busy?)
- Location: **¿Dónde está el baño?** · **Estoy en casa.**
- Quick rule: **ser = what it is · estar = how or where it is.** Compare **Es aburrido** (he's boring) with **Está aburrido** (he's bored).
- Make any sentence negative by putting **no** before the verb: **No estoy cansado.**

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. I'm at home.
2. Where is the dog?
3. We're busy today.
4. Are you sad?
5. My friends are here.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Estoy en casa.
> 2. ¿Dónde está el perro?
> 3. Hoy estamos ocupados.
> 4. ¿Estás triste?
> 5. Mis amigos están aquí.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿Dónde estás ahora? ¿Qué hay aquí?** _(Where are you right now? What's here?)_
- **¿Cómo estás hoy: cansado, contento, enfermo?** _(How are you today: tired, happy, sick?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.

Afterwards, add the best correction you got to [[Spanish Mistakes Log]].

> Answer the questions out loud anyway and record yourself on your phone. Listen back once and note one thing to fix. Or book a session with a tutor (italki, Preply) or a language exchange partner (Tandem, HelloTalk).
