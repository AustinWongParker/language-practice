## 2 · Learn (8 min)
**Ser** (to be) is for what something *is*: identity, where it's from, job, and lasting traits.

| | ser |
|---|---|
| yo (I) | **soy** |
| tú (you) | **eres** |
| él / ella / usted (he / she / you formal) | **es** |
| nosotros / nosotras (we) | **somos** |
| vosotros (you all, Spain only) | **sois** |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes (they / you all) | **son** |

- **Soy de Estados Unidos.** = I'm from the United States. **¿De dónde eres?** = Where are you from?
- Jobs take no "a": **Soy programador** (not *un programador*). For a woman: **programadora**.
- Spanish usually drops the pronoun, because the verb already says who: **Soy Austin** is enough.
- **Vosotros** is only used in Spain. Recognize it, but you don't need to practice it.

## 3 · Listen (7 min)
Watch a **Superbeginner** video on [Dreaming Spanish](https://www.dreaming.com/spanish) (or its [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@DreamingSpanish) channel). Don't translate in your head; follow along from the pictures and gestures. The site logs your hours for you.
**Listen for:** es and son when people describe someone.

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. I'm a programmer.
2. Where are you from?
3. We are friends.
4. She is very nice.
5. They are students from Mexico.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Soy programador (programadora).
> 2. ¿De dónde eres?
> 3. Somos amigos.
> 4. Ella es muy simpática.
> 5. Son estudiantes de México.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿Quién eres? ¿De dónde eres?** _(Who are you? Where are you from?)_
- **¿Cuál es tu trabajo? ¿Y el trabajo de tu mejor amigo?** _(What do you do? And your best friend?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.

Afterwards, add the best correction you got to [[Spanish Mistakes Log]].

> Answer the questions out loud anyway and record yourself on your phone. Listen back once and note one thing to fix. Or book a session with a tutor (italki, Preply) or a language exchange partner (Tandem, HelloTalk).
