## 2 · Learn (8 min)
| para | por |
|---|---|
| purpose: **Estudio para aprender.** (in order to) | reason, cause: **Gracias por la ayuda.** (because of) |
| recipient: **Es para ti.** | through, along: **Camino por el parque.** |
| destination: **Salgo para Madrid.** | exchange, price: **Lo compré por diez dólares.** |
| deadline: **para el lunes** (by Monday) | means: **por teléfono** (by phone) |

Fixed phrases with **por**: **por favor** · **por la mañana** · **por fin** · **por eso** (that's why)

**Topic words** (used in this grammar point; the frequency words come next)

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| para | for, in order to |
| por | for, through, because of |
| la ayuda | help |

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. This gift is for you.
2. Thanks for the help.
3. I'm studying Spanish to travel.
4. We walked through the park.
5. I bought it for ten dollars.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Este regalo es para ti.
> 2. Gracias por la ayuda.
> 3. Estudio español para viajar.
> 4. Caminamos por el parque.
> 5. Lo compré por diez dólares.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿Para qué estudias español? ¿Por cuánto tiempo?** _(What are you studying Spanish for? For how long?)_
- **¿Gracias por qué? Dime tres cosas.** _(Thanks for what? Tell me three things.)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.

Afterwards, add the best correction you got to [[Spanish Mistakes Log]].

> Answer the questions out loud anyway and record yourself on your phone. Listen back once and note one thing to fix. Or book a session with a tutor (italki, Preply) or a language exchange partner (Tandem, HelloTalk).
