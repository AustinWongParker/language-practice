## 2 · Learn (8 min)
| preterite: what happened | imperfect: what was going on |
|---|---|
| finished events | background and description |
| a specific moment, or a number of times | habits, "used to" |
| a sequence: first this, then that | an action in progress ("was …-ing") |
| clue words: **ayer, una vez, de repente** | clue words: **siempre, todos los días, mientras** |

They often appear together: the imperfect sets the scene, the preterite interrupts it.
**Leía cuando sonó el teléfono.** = I was reading when the phone rang.

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. I was reading when the phone rang.
2. It was raining, so we stayed home.
3. Every day I walked, but yesterday I ran.
4. While she cooked, I cleaned.
5. Suddenly, the dog came in.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Leía cuando sonó el teléfono.
> 2. Llovía, así que nos quedamos en casa.
> 3. Todos los días caminaba, pero ayer corrí.
> 4. Mientras ella cocinaba, yo limpiaba.
> 5. De repente, el perro entró.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **Cuéntame un cuento corto.** _(Tell me a short story.)_
- **¿Qué pasaba cuando sonó tu teléfono?** _(What was happening when your phone rang?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.

Afterwards, add the best correction you got to [[Spanish Mistakes Log]].
