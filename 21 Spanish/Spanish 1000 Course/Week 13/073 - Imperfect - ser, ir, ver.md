## 2 · Learn (8 min)
Only three verbs are irregular in the imperfect:

| | ser | ir | ver |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | **era** | **iba** | **veía** |
| tú | **eras** | **ibas** | **veías** |
| él / ella / usted | **era** | **iba** | **veía** |
| nosotros | **éramos** | **íbamos** | **veíamos** |
| ellos / ustedes | **eran** | **iban** | **veían** |

- Describing the past: **Era muy tímido.** · **La casa era pequeña.**
- Time and age in the past: **Eran las diez.** · **Tenía diez años.**
- **Cuando era niño (niña)…** = When I was a kid…

## 4 · Talk with your partner (10 min)
See the [[Conversation Partner Guide]] for how to run this.

**Warm-up drill (3 min):** your partner reads the English; you say it in Spanish. They check the answer key.

1. When I was a kid, I was very shy.
2. We used to go to the beach every summer.
3. I used to watch cartoons.
4. It was ten o'clock.
5. My school was small.

> [!check]- Answer key (for your partner)
> 1. Cuando era niño (niña), era muy tímido (tímida).
> 2. Íbamos a la playa todos los veranos.
> 3. Veía dibujos animados.
> 4. Eran las diez.
> 5. Mi escuela era pequeña.

**Conversation (7 min):** your partner asks, you answer in full sentences. Try to use at least three of today's words.

- **¿Cómo era tu escuela?** _(What was your school like?)_
- **¿Quién era tu amigo cuando eras joven?** _(Who was your friend when you were young?)_
- Then switch: you ask your partner the same questions.

Afterwards, add the best correction you got to [[Spanish Mistakes Log]].
